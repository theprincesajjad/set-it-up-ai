## 1. Who this is for

- **The shops.** One or two locations, an owner who works the floor, two to eight staff, 1,500 to 6,000 sq ft. Four common shapes: (a) South Asian grocers (Brampton, Mississauga, Scarborough, Gerrard India Bazaar) selling 20 lb bags of atta and rice, dal, ghee, spices, sweets and festival goods; (b) halal butchers and grocers with a meat counter (goat, lamb, beef, chicken cut to order, often zabihah), common in the same corridors and across Middle Eastern, Afghan, Somali and Bengali communities; (c) Caribbean, Chinese, Korean and Vietnamese grocers with seasonal peaks (Lunar New Year, Mid-Autumn and Chuseok, black cake season); (d) organic shops and corner or convenience stores (milk, bread, tobacco, lottery, a lottery-and-bills counter).
- **Tools today.** A till or POS (Square, Clover, Lightspeed, or a local grocery POS with a scale), a legal-for-trade scale at the meat counter and deli, a WhatsApp number that people message with a list, a phone, a printed or Instagram flyer, a paper "khata" (account book) for regulars. No customer list they can message.
- **The money.** Gross margin about 27% for independents (W: CFIG and FMS survey, [CFIG](https://cfig.ca/blog/2019/11/07/independent-grocers-hold-their-own-in-tough-environment-cfig-fms-study/)); net margin for small grocers around 2% at the median (W: [ISED financial performance, NAICS 4451](https://ised-isde.canada.ca/app/ixb/cis/performance/4451), 2024 quartile breakpoints of roughly -0.3%, 2.1% and 8.3%, hard to parse, treat as approximate). A grocer cannot give 5% back.
- **Customers.** Families who shop for the household and the extended family: a weekly top-up plus a monthly bulk run, and a big festival shop. Many are first-generation, 55+, use WhatsApp and the phone, and read in Hindi, Urdu or Punjabi as often as English. Shopping is frequent and small: about 5.4 grocery visits a month (W: [Dalhousie Agri-Food Analytics Lab with Caddle, reported by Canadian Grocer](https://canadiangrocer.com/how-canadian-grocery-habits-are-shifting-more-trips-smaller-baskets)).

## 3. Jobs to be done (ranked by frequency x pain)

1. **Get the same family back this week.** (Owner, weekly.) A reward small enough to afford that people can see getting closer. Already served by the points template; changed to be affordable (section 4).
2. **Put this week's deals in front of regulars.** (Owner, weekly, peak before festivals.) A flyer that goes on WhatsApp, the shop page and a text, with the festival stock-up on time.
3. **Take the butcher's order the way customers already give it.** (Owner and counter, daily.) "Two goats' legs and chicken for Saturday 11" by phone or WhatsApp becomes an order with a pickup time, a deposit where needed and a place on the cutting list.
4. **Price by weight without surprises.** (Counter, every meat sale.) Show an estimate, weigh at the scale, charge what it says, tell the customer.
5. **Survive Eid al-Adha.** (Owner, one or two weeks a year, the biggest.) Whole animals and cow shares with a deposit, a cap per pick-up day, a wait list, and a way to move the plan when the moon moves it.
6. **Do not lose the monthly bulk run.** (Owner, monthly.) The household that buys a 20 lb bag every six weeks buys it somewhere else the week we do not remind them.
7. **Keep tobacco, lottery, bills and alcohol out of the points.** (Counter, every sale.) Rules and margin: they never earn.
8. **Answer WhatsApp lists without retyping.** (Owner and counter, daily.) An inbox where a list becomes an order.
9. **Speak the customer's language.** (All.) English, Hindi, Urdu, Punjabi on every customer text and page.
10. **Do it on the till they have.** (Counter.) No new hardware; a phone or tablet at the till.

## 5. What we deliberately don't build

- **Delivery and routes.** A route needs a driver and a margin we did not find; the owner already delivers by phone. Revisit with a pilot shop.
- **Item-level inventory and a POS.** Grocers have one; we sit beside it. No stock counts, no barcode scanning.
- **Percent-back or tiered "VIP" rewards for grocers.** The margin does not support it.
- **A flyer publisher to Flipp or print.** The shop page, WhatsApp share and a text cover the job; a Flipp listing is outside our control and its price is unknown.
- **Automatic reminders without a recorded yes**, and any reminder at night.
- **Halal or zabihah certification claims.** The text is the shop's; we never display a certificate.
- **A scale integration.** The cashier types what the approved scale reads.
- **Mobile app screens for the owner.** Web only at launch.
- **A separate "butcher" vertical.** One `grocery` vertical covers a butcher counter inside a grocer and a butcher alone; the features turn on per shop.

## 7. Build spec

- **Shared** (`packages/shared`): `grocery.ts` (weight math, per-unit price, `rewardBackPct`, pantry cadence, season calendar), `api/grocery.ts` (zod bodies and DTOs), four `grocery.*` features with `FeatureDef.vertical`, 20 `gr.*` strings in en, hi, ur, pa.
- **DB** (`packages/db`): columns on `catalog_items`, `order_items`, `orders` (weight, unit price, taxable, `weighedAt`); tables `grocery_deals`, `pantry_items`, `pantry_entries`, `pantry_reminders`; migration `0023_groc`.
- **API**: endpoints and rules in `docs/engineering/api/grocery.md`; job `grocery.pantry_reminders`.
- **Web**: owner home, Weekly flyer, Butcher counter (with Eid plan), Pantry reminders, WhatsApp pages under `/m/:id/grocery/*`; counter extras; rewards cost line; shop-page flyer and butcher blocks; pass "your pantry".
- **Seed**: `apps/api/src/seed/grocers.ts`.
- **Acceptance checks**: API tests for every endpoint and the job (`apps/api/test/grocery.test.ts`), shared and web unit tests, `apps/web/e2e/grocers.spec.ts`, the full `e2e:full` run green.
- **Decisions at final verify**: the flyer shows "$1.37/100 g" as in Figma frames 04 and 10 (the shop-page string is `{price}/100 g` in all four languages); the Eid plan names its days "Day 1", "Day 2" under the date; counter pantry logging runs as the PIN-switched staff member (`counter.logPantry`, and the BFF forwards the staff token for `/grocery/counter/` paths); a butcher weigh-in below the deposit refunds the excess at once (card refund, or a recorded manual refund for e-transfer or cash).
- **Closed in the v4 pass**: the restock text (frame 12) now opens a one-item reserve page (`/shops/<slug>/restock/<pantryItemId>`: prefilled item and price, a pick-up window, Reserve, the reminder switch), which is the ordinary pickup order for one unit paid at the counter; qurbani pre-order days are the shop's Eid plan days (moon-sighting adjusted, each with its own animal cap and notice), and a qurbani order for another day is refused (`qurbani_day`).
- **Open items**: no grocer screens in the Expo app; WhatsApp template credits for grocers are not defined, so reminders and flyers go by SMS; pantry cadence and margin figures are estimates; qurbani prices in the demo are samples; hi, ur and pa strings need a native reader, including the two butcher pre-order notice lines (`gr.pre.notice`, `gr.pre.orderBy`) added in the final pass.
