## 1. Who this is for

**The businesses.** Owner-run Pakistani, North and South Indian, Hyderabadi, Punjabi dhaba, Bangladeshi, Sri Lankan/Tamil and Afghan restaurants, plus mithai and chai shops, in strips and plazas: Ridgeway Plaza (Mississauga), Brampton (Queen, Bramalea, Steeles), Malton, Scarborough (Markham/Lawrence, Kennedy), and the Gerrard India Bazaar in Toronto. Gerrard India Bazaar is described as 100 to 135+ small businesses, mixed restaurants, groceries and clothing, with Indian, Pakistani, Bangladeshi, Nepali and Sri Lankan restaurants [S: [Streets of Toronto](https://streetsoftoronto.com/little-india-neighbourhood-guide/), [Curiocity](https://curiocity.com/diwali-mela-festival-toronto/), no restaurant-only count found]. Brampton is described as having the GTA's largest Indian immigrant population and Punjabi restaurants as some of its most popular [S: [BlogTO](https://www.blogto.com/eat_drink/2022/10/indian-restaurants-brampton)].

**How they run [O].**

- 2 to 4 owners or family members on the floor; 4 to 12 staff. Kitchen staff often speak Urdu, Hindi or Punjabi first; front of house mixes English with those. A Manchester study of the Curry Mile found the same kitchen/front split (kitchen Urdu, front of house English) [S: [Curry Mile study](https://kratylos.org/~raphael/multilingual/wp-content/uploads/2015/12/Language-use-in-business-on-the-Curry-Mile-in-Manchester.pdf)]; Toronto-specific data on kitchen languages was not found.
- Stations: curry/gravy, rice and dum (deghs), tandoor (naan, tikka, kebab), grill/BBQ, sweets and chai. Biryani is cooked in large pots (deghs) sealed with dough and finished on dum (about 60 to 90 minutes) [O]. The kitchen already runs on paper tickets and shouting.
- Order channels: counter and walk-in, phone (rings through the dinner rush), WhatsApp on the shop's own number, then Uber Eats, DoorDash and SkipTheDishes, and a basic website or none [O]. Many orders arrive as voice notes or typed Roman Urdu/Hindi ("do plate nihari, 8 baje") [O].
- Money: card and Interac at the counter, a lot of cash, e-Transfer for tiffin and catering deposits (free to the restaurant, per combined-strategy §2.2 [A in that doc]). Ontario allows merchants to pass on card costs after notice to the card networks and disclosure; reported cap on surcharges is 2.4% [S: [CTV](https://toronto.ctvnews.ca/what-people-in-ontario-need-to-know-about-new-credit-card-surcharges-1.6097101), CFIB figure as reported]. A "cash discount" is presented as a lower price, not a surcharge, and is more common at restaurants than surcharging [S: [Paystone](https://www.paystone.com/resources/should-you-add-a-credit-card-surcharge-the-canadian-business-owners-honest-guide)].
- POS: Square, Clover, TouchBistro, Lightspeed and Menusifu are all sold into Canadian restaurants [S: [Menusifu](https://www.menusifu.com/support) serves US and Canada]; many Desi strips run a cheap terminal plus paper tickets [O]. We do not replace the POS (see §5).
- Seasons: Ramadan and Eid, Diwali, Vaisakhi, Navratri, Gurpurab, Lohri, Bangladesh Victory Day, plus summer wedding season for catering and banquet halls [O]. Projected 2027 dates: Ramadan about Feb 8, Eid al-Fitr about Mar 9, Eid al-Adha about May 16 or 17, all by moon sighting and possibly a day off in Canada [S: [timeanddate](https://timeanddate.com/holidays/canada/ramadan-begins), [Khaleej Times](https://www.khaleejtimes.com/uae/ramadan-eid-al-fitr-eid-al-adha-2027-likely-dates)]. Diwali 2026 is Sunday Nov 8 in our calendar (approximate, lunar).
- Reviews: Google is the storefront. A few unanswered 1-star reviews cost a plaza restaurant walk-ins; owners rarely reply, and rarely in the reviewer's language [O].
- Loyalty today: paper stamp cards and "regulars" the owner knows by name [O]. Chowbus was founded to expand the customer base of "mom and pop" Asian restaurants and offers a points-based rewards program on its POS [S: [Capterra CA](https://www.capterra.ca/software/1061050/chowbus-pos), job posting].

**The guests.** Families of 4 to 15 ordering for Friday and Sunday, young professionals ordering for pickup, aunties and uncles who order on the phone or WhatsApp, and event hosts (mehndi, walima, aqiqah, Eid, Diwali, office lunch). Languages: English, Urdu, Hindi, Punjabi, Bengali, Tamil. The 2011 census backgrounder showed Urdu, Gujarati and Bengali among Toronto's top Indo-Aryan home languages [S: [Toronto.ca backgrounder](https://www.toronto.ca/wp-content/uploads/2017/10/8fab-2011-Census-Backgrounder-Language.pdf)]. Orders are decided in family WhatsApp groups, so a link must work with no app [O].

### 2.3 Halal: certifiers, claims and what "zabihah" requires

- **Federal rule.** Since April 2016, a halal claim on labels, packaging and advertising must be backed by certification and name the certifier [S: [CFIA/Canada.ca 2014 notice](https://www.canada.ca/en/news/archive/2014/06/new-halal-labelling-requirements-benefit-consumers-food-producers.html), [Canada Gazette](https://gazette.gc.ca/rp-pr/p1/2013/2013-06-01/html/reg1-eng.html)]. CFIA lists monitoring authorities but does not regulate their practices; anyone can become a certifier [S: [Canadian Pizza](https://www.canadianpizzamag.com/halal-labelling-rules-kick-in-today-but-certifying-organizations-remain-unregulated-6509/)]. We found no Ontario rule specific to restaurants; whether the federal advertising rule reaches a restaurant menu was not confirmed, so we treat "name the certifier" as best practice, not as a legal guarantee.
- **Over-claiming is real.** CBC Marketplace visited 10 GTA chain locations: staff at six said the whole restaurant was halal-certified; none was [S: [CBC](https://www.cbc.ca/1.7352523)].
- **Bodies.** Halal Monitoring Authority (HMA), Scarborough, listed on a federal annex of Islamic bodies; its application form requires hand slaughter, no stunning, Muslim slaughtermen, Muslim workers on the monitored line, traceability and supplier approval [S: [HMA form](https://hmacanada.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/HMA-Application-Form-Other-Products-2024.pdf)]. The Islamic Food and Nutrition Council of Canada (IFANCC), Mississauga, is on the same annex [S: [CFIA annex](https://inspection.canada.ca/en/exporting-food-plants-animals/food-exports/requirements/malaysia-meat-and-poultry/annex-c)]. ISNA Canada appears in consumer guides, its restaurant scope unconfirmed [S: [Welcome Aide](https://welcomeaide.com/zh-Hans/blog/halal-food-grocery-stores-canada-cities)]. The seed data's certifier names (Crescent Halal Council, Al-Amanah) are fictional.
- **"Zabihah" in practice [O, consistent with the HMA standard above].** Hand slaughter without stunning, by a Muslim, with a recitation; the claim only holds if the supplier chain is traceable. "Halal" alone may include machine slaughter. Guests ask three things: is the meat zabihah, who certifies, and is the kitchen shared with non-halal food. The product must let an owner state all three truthfully and must never claim anything for them.

### 2.4 WhatsApp

- Meta moved to per-message pricing on July 1, 2025, with categories marketing, utility, authentication and service; service replies inside the 24-hour window are free; utility and authentication rates for Canada and the US fell on Jan 1, 2026 [S: [Meta pricing](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/pricing), [WATI](https://support.wati.io/en/articles/11561662-message-based-pricing-all-you-need-to-know)]. A Salesforce-hosted rate card (Aug 1, 2025) lists Canada at 30.87 for marketing and 4.94 for utility, with units not stated [S: [rate card PDF](https://www.salesforce.com/en-us/wp-content/uploads/sites/4/assets/pdf/WhatsApp_Business-Messaging-Rate-Card-Aug-1-2025.pdf)]. **Action: verify Meta's current Canada marketing and utility rates before selling a "1,000 messages for $49" pack (4.9 cents a message). If the marketing rate is above that, broadcast-heavy restaurants lose money.**
- Quiet hours (no sends 21:00 to 08:00) and STOP handling already exist in the code (restaurant-endpoints.md).

### 2.5 Google reviews

Google's policy, as summarised by several vendors, prohibits discouraging negative reviews or selectively soliciting positive ones; the safe practice is to ask every customer the same way [S: [Seologist](https://seologist.com/knowledge-sharing/what-is-review-gating-and-why-does-it-violate-googles-review-policies/), [Vendasta](https://vendasta.com/blog/review-gating); not Google's own page: confirm wording at Google's support site]. Our `services/reviews.ts` shows 4 and 5 stars a "lead with Google" response and 1 to 3 stars a softer one. That is a risk (finding 3).

## 5. What we deliberately don't build

- **A POS or inventory.** Owners keep Square, Clover or TouchBistro. We show a per-pot recipe (rice and meat kilos) for the pre-order list only.
- **Delivery-app integration or our own couriers.** Not promised in the UI. Delivery stays with the apps; our job is moving regulars to direct links.
- **Verifying halal.** We store the owner's certificate and expiry, print the certifier as given, and never badge a shop ourselves. CFIA does not regulate certifiers, so a verified badge from us would be a liability.
- **Importing Google reviews.** No connector; Reviews shows our own answers, not a "Google rating".
- **Dine-in QR table ordering and table reservations.** Not in the top-10 jobs; revisit after the 20 interviews.
- **Auto-sent festival messages.** Owners review and schedule every send, because lunar dates move.
- **A separate Desi consumer brand.** One network (combined-strategy).
- **Voice assistant as a required path.** Phone assistant stays a $79 add-on or Pro; manual phone ordering works on every tier.
