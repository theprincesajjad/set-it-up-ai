# Local Loyalty — Rewards Terms

Last updated: [date] · Version [0.1-draft]

These Rewards Terms explain how points work on Local Loyalty. They form part of the Customer Terms of Service.

## 1. The short version

1. **Your points never expire.** There is no expiry for time passing and no expiry for inactivity.
2. **Points from a shop are redeemable at that shop**, at the reward values shown in the app and on the counter display.
3. **Network Rewards** (bonuses funded by Local Loyalty, marked with the network badge) are redeemable at **any** participating shop that accepts Network Rewards, as shown in the app.
4. You can always see your balance, your history and what you can redeem today, for every shop, in the app or wallet pass, or by asking at the counter.
5. If a shop changes its program, points you have already earned keep their value for at least **90 days**, and you are told in advance.
6. Points are not cash. They cannot be bought, sold or transferred, and they cannot be exchanged for cash except where the law requires.

## 2. Who issues your points

- **Merchant points** are issued and funded by the merchant where you earned them. The merchant sets the earn rules (per dollar, per visit, stamps or items) and the reward catalog. Each merchant's balance is separate.
- **Network Rewards** are issued and funded by **[Local Loyalty Inc.]**. Merchants never pay for Network Rewards. When you redeem one at a shop, Local Loyalty reimburses that shop.
- **Campaign points** come from neighbourhood campaigns, such as a BIA points weekend. They are funded by the campaign sponsor and shown with the sponsor's name. They are redeemable as described in the campaign rules, which are visible before the campaign starts, and **they never expire** either.

## 3. Earning

- You earn when the merchant records your visit or purchase: by phone number, by scanning your pass, or automatically from a connected point-of-sale system. Ask staff to record it at the time of purchase. Missing points can be added by the merchant within **30 days** with a receipt.
- **Excluded items.** Some products cannot earn or be redeemed against, by law or by merchant choice. They are shown as "no-earn".
  - **At pharmacies, prescriptions and pharmacy services never earn points and cannot be paid for with points**, as required by Ontario pharmacy rules. Only front-store purchases count. A pharmacy gives no points for a visit or a stamp, no reward for referring a friend, and does not carry over points from another program.
  - **Alcohol, tobacco, vaping, cannabis and lottery products do not earn** unless the app shows otherwise for a specific shop, and only where permitted by law.
- Points for returned or refunded purchases are removed. If you have already redeemed them, your balance may go negative until you earn again; we never charge you money for a negative balance.
- Bonuses (welcome, birthday, double-points days, referrals, network perks) follow the rules shown on each offer. Referral bonuses go to real new members only.

## 4. Redeeming

- Redeem at the counter by phone number or pass, or in the app. The merchant must honour any reward displayed as available in your app at the moment of redemption, provided the item is in stock. If an item is unavailable, the merchant offers an equivalent reward or you keep your points.
- **Minimums and limits.** A reward costs the number of points shown. No hidden minimum balance, blackout dates or "available only on" rules apply unless they are shown on the reward itself *before* you earn toward it.
- Rewards cannot be combined with other offers if the reward says so.
- Taxes are applied as required by law.

## 5. Changes to a program

A merchant may change its earn rates or reward catalog going forward. When a change would make an existing reward cost more points or would remove a reward:

- you are notified in the app at least **30 days** before the change;
- for at least **90 days** you can redeem points you already hold at the **old** values.

Local Loyalty will not change the value of Network Rewards you already hold.

## 6. If a shop leaves the network or closes

- **The merchant leaves Local Loyalty.** It must keep honouring your points for at least **60 days** after you are notified. Your balance and the last day to redeem are shown in the app, and we send you a reminder.
- **The merchant closes permanently without notice.** We will tell you as soon as we know. We cannot guarantee value issued by a closed business.
- **Network Rewards** are unaffected; use them at any other participating shop.

## 7. If Local Loyalty shuts down

We will give at least **90 days' notice**:

- Merchant points remain redeemable directly with each merchant, which can export its member balances.
- Network Rewards can be redeemed during the notice period.

## 8. Closing your account

You can close your account at any time. We show your balances first so you can redeem them. Closed-account points are cancelled 30 days after closure; you can reopen within that window. We do not cancel points of open accounts for inactivity.

## 9. Errors and fraud

- We or the merchant may correct clear errors, such as a duplicate award or a mistyped amount, and you will see the correction in your history.
- Points obtained by fraud may be removed, after we tell you the reason and give you a chance to respond, except where the law or an investigation prevents that.

## 10. Questions and complaints

Ask the shop, or contact [support email]. We aim to resolve points disputes within **10 business days**. If a merchant refuses a valid redemption, tell us; we will follow up with the merchant, and we track this as part of merchant compliance.
