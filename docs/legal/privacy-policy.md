# Local Loyalty — Privacy Policy

Last updated: [date] · Version [0.1-draft]

**[Local Loyalty Inc.]** ("we") runs a shared loyalty network for independent shops. This policy explains:

- what personal information we collect;
- why we collect it;
- who sees it;
- where it is stored;
- your choices.

**Privacy Officer:** [name], [email], [address]

## 1. The short version

- We collect the minimum needed to run your loyalty membership: your **mobile number**, **first name**, optional **birthday (day and month only)**, and your **visits, points and redemptions**.
- **Each shop sees only your activity at that shop.** Shops never see where else you shop.
- **We do not sell your personal information.** BIA and neighbourhood reports are aggregated and never identify you.
- Marketing messages are opt-in, **separately for each shop**, and you can unsubscribe at any time.
- We store our main database in **Canada** where practical. Some service providers, such as payments and SMS, may process data in the US.
- We **never collect prescription or health information**. At pharmacies only front-store purchases count.

## 2. What we collect

| Information | Where it comes from | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mobile number, first name, email (optional), birthday month and day (optional) | You, at the counter, in the app or on a wallet pass | Create your account, verify your number, birthday reward |
| Visits, amounts recorded, points, redemptions, rewards | Participating shops, including their POS systems if connected | Run each shop's program and Network Rewards |
| Consent records: what you agreed to, when, and how | You | Prove consent (CASL) and respect your choices |
| Orders, quotes, approvals, deposits, invoices | You and the shop | Provide order-ahead and service features |
| Payment details | Entered directly with **Stripe**; we receive only limited information such as card brand and last 4 digits | Process payments; we never store full card numbers |
| Approximate or precise location (only if you allow it) | Your device | Show nearby shops and nearby promos you opted into |
| Device and usage data: app version, crash logs, IP address | Your device | Security, fraud prevention, fixing bugs |
| Messages with support | You | Help you |

We do not collect government ID, health information, precise location history or your contacts.

## 3. How we use it

We use your information to:

- provide the Service;
- run each shop's program and Network Rewards;
- send service messages (verification codes, reward-ready and order-ready notices);
- send promotional messages **only with your consent**;
- prevent fraud and abuse;
- improve the Service;
- produce **aggregated, de-identified** statistics for shops and Business Improvement Areas, for example "visits on the street rose 18% during the passport week", reported only in groups of [10] or more people;
- meet legal obligations.

If we want to use your information for a new purpose, we will ask you first.

## 4. Who sees it

- **Each participating shop** sees your name, number, and your visits, points and redemptions **at that shop**, plus your marketing preference for that shop. A shop can export this list to manage its own customer relationship; it is then responsible for using it lawfully.
- **Local Loyalty staff** see it only as needed for support, security and operations, with role-based access and audit logs.
- **Service providers** process data for us under contract and only on our instructions:
  - hosting and database: [provider, Canadian region];
  - Stripe (payments);
  - Twilio (SMS);
  - [email provider];
  - Apple and Google (wallet passes and push notifications);
  - [analytics and crash reporting].

  Some are located in, or may process data in, the **United States** or other countries, where it may be accessible to authorities under local law.
- **BIAs and sponsors** receive aggregated reports only, never your personal information.
- **Legal reasons.** We disclose information where required by law, or to protect rights, safety or prevent fraud.
- **Business transfer.** If our business is sold, your information may transfer under equivalent protections, and you will be notified.

**We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it with data brokers.**

## 5. Your choices

- **Marketing:** opt in or out per shop and for Local Loyalty, by email, SMS (reply STOP) or push, in settings. Unsubscribes take effect promptly and within 10 business days at most.
- **Mute a shop**, or **leave a shop's program**. Your points at that shop stay unless you close your account.
- **Location:** turn it off in device settings.
- **Access and correction:** see and edit your profile in the app, or ask us. We respond within **30 days**.
- **Export and deletion:** download your data or delete your account in settings. Some records, such as payment and tax records or fraud logs, are kept as long as the law requires.
- **Withdraw consent** at any time, subject to legal or contractual limits. We will explain what that means for the Service.

## 6. Retention

- We keep account information while your account is open.
- If an account has been inactive for **[3] years**, we notify you and then delete or anonymize personal information. Before that, we give you the chance to redeem or keep your points.
  - Counsel: reconcile this with points that never expire, for example by keeping a minimal balance record.
- Consent records are kept for at least [3] years after the relationship ends. Transaction records are kept as required for tax purposes ([6] years).

## 7. Security

- Encryption in transit and at rest.
- Role-based access, and multi-factor authentication for staff.
- Logging and monitoring.
- Vendor security review.
- Card data is handled only by Stripe (PCI DSS).

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach creates a real risk of significant harm, we will notify you and the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada as the law requires, and we keep records of all breaches.

## 8. Children

The Service is for people aged **[16]** and over. We do not knowingly collect information from younger children; contact us if you think we have.

## 9. Changes

We will notify you in the app or by email at least 30 days before material changes take effect.

## 10. Questions and complaints

Contact our Privacy Officer at [email]. If you are not satisfied, you can complain to the **Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada** (priv.gc.ca).
