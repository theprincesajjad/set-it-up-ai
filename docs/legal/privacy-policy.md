# Local Loyalty — Privacy Policy

Last updated: [date] · Version [0.1-draft]

**[Local Loyalty Inc.]** ("we") runs a shared loyalty network for independent shops. This policy explains:

- what personal information we collect;
- why we collect it;
- who sees it;
- where it is stored;
- your choices.

**Privacy Officer:** [name], [email], [address]

## 1. The short version

- We collect the minimum needed to run your loyalty membership: your **mobile number**, **first name**, optional **birthday (day and month only)**, and your **visits, points and redemptions**.
- **Each shop sees only your activity at that shop.** Shops never see where else you shop.
- **We do not sell your personal information.** BIA and neighbourhood reports are aggregated and never identify you.
- Marketing messages are opt-in, **separately for each shop**, and you can unsubscribe at any time.
- We store our main database in **Canada** where practical. Some service providers, such as payments and SMS, may process data in the US.
- We **never collect prescription or health records**. At pharmacies only front-store purchases count. If you tell a restaurant about an allergy, that note goes only to that restaurant.
- Features like orders, appointments, tiffin delivery, catering, WhatsApp and phone ordering collect more, only when you use them. Section 2 lists everything.

## 3. How we use it

We use your information to:

- provide the Service and the features you use;
- run each shop's program and Network Rewards;
- send service messages (verification codes, reward-ready, order-ready, booking confirmations and reminders, delivery updates, quote and invoice notices);
- send promotional messages **only with your consent**;
- answer questions with our **AI assistant** (see below);
- draft an order from a phone call or WhatsApp message for a shop to confirm (a person confirms; nothing is charged because of the draft alone);
- prevent fraud and abuse;
- improve the Service;
- produce **aggregated, de-identified** statistics for shops and Business Improvement Areas, for example "visits on the street rose 18% during the passport week", reported only in groups of 10 or more people;
- meet legal obligations.

**Our AI assistant.** On our website and on our own WhatsApp number an AI assistant answers questions about {{plan.free.name}}, the paid plans, fees and set-up, in English, Hindi, Urdu and Punjabi. It tells you it is an AI in its first message and you can ask for a person at any time by typing HUMAN. It can look up our prices and policies, save your details as a lead, send you a sign-up link, note a request for a call with our founder, and, only after you prove you own a shop by typing a code we text to your phone, show you your own shop's plan and create a checkout link for it. It cannot see any other shop or person. It never decides anything about you by itself: it cannot approve, refuse or change a price, and anything it is not sure about goes to a person. To run it, the text of the chat is processed by our service provider **Anthropic** (the AI model), which may process it in the United States. Under our agreement Anthropic may not use these chats to train its models, and neither do we. Do not put card numbers, passwords or health information in the chat. Reply STOP on WhatsApp and we stop messaging that number.

If we want to use your information for a new purpose, we will ask you first.

## 5. Your choices

- **Marketing:** opt in or out per shop and for Local Loyalty, by email, SMS (reply STOP, or ARRET in French), WhatsApp (reply STOP) or push, in settings. Unsubscribes take effect promptly and within 10 business days at most. Replying STOP ends marketing from every shop and from us. Service messages about an order or booking you made continue.
- **Mute a shop**, or **leave a shop's program**. Your points at that shop stay unless you close your account.
- **Location:** turn it off in device settings.
- **Access and correction:** see and edit your profile in the app, or ask us. We respond within **30 days**.
- **Export and deletion:** download your data from settings (a JSON file with your profile, every shop's activity and everything you gave a feature: orders, appointments, tickets, tiffin, catering, WhatsApp, review feedback, wait lists, consents and more), or delete your account there. Deleting your account removes your name, phone, email, birthday, postal code and consent records, signs you out everywhere, and clears what you gave each feature: order and allergy notes, appointment notes, tiffin addresses and notes, catering and hall event details and signatures, WhatsApp chats, phone transcripts, review comments, repair details, photos and quote signatures, wait-list entries, referral links, wallet passes and push tokens. Records the law or accounting needs, such as paid invoices, charges and deposits, stay with only amounts and dates and no longer identify you; fraud logs are kept as long as the law requires. We keep one entry for a number that replied STOP, so we never message it again.
- **Withdraw consent** at any time, subject to legal or contractual limits. We will explain what that means for the Service.

## 6. Retention

- We keep account information while your account is open.
- If an account has been inactive for **3 years**, we notify you and then delete or anonymize personal information. Before that, we give you the chance to redeem or keep your points.
- Consent records are kept for at least 3 years after the relationship ends. Transaction records are kept as required for tax purposes (6 years).
- **Phone-call transcripts:** kept for {{retention.phoneTranscriptDays}} after the call, then deleted, together with the order text we drafted from it and the caller's number. The confirmed order itself stays.
- **WhatsApp messages:** message text is deleted {{retention.whatsappMessageDays}} after it was sent. Carts and allergy notes on a chat that has gone quiet are cleared at the same time.
- **AI assistant chats:** the messages, the record of what the assistant looked up or did, and the number are deleted {{retention.salesAgentDays}} after the last message. A lead we saved for you follows the rules for leads, not this one.
- **Repair photos and ticket records:** kept for the life of the ticket and {{retention.repairPhotoMonths}} after it closes (picked up or cancelled, the warranty and dispute period). Then the photos (the files and the links), item details, notes and the quote signature record are deleted. Quote and invoice amounts stay for the shop's accounts.
- **Allergy and dietary notes:** an allergy note on an order is cleared {{retention.allergyNoteDays}} after the order is picked up or cancelled. Dietary notes on a tiffin subscription follow the tiffin rule below. Allergy and dietary notes are not used for marketing or analytics.
- **Review feedback** (your private comment and the shop's reply) is deleted {{retention.reviewFeedbackMonths}} after it was given. The star rating stays and does not identify you.
- **Tiffin addresses and driver links:** a driver link stops working the same night and its record is deleted after {{retention.driverLinkDays}}. Your address, postal code and delivery and dietary notes are kept while your subscription is active and for {{retention.tiffinAddressMonths}} after it ends.
- **Location:** we do not keep a location history.
- **Appointments:** notes and the name typed at the desk are deleted {{retention.appointmentMonths}} after the visit. The booking and its payment record stay.
- **Unpaid holds:** a booking or hall hold that lapsed without payment is deleted {{retention.abandonedHoldDays}} later.
- These deletions run automatically, several times a day.

## 7. Security

- Encryption in transit and at rest.
- Role-based access, and multi-factor authentication for staff.
- Logging and monitoring.
- Vendor security review.
- Card data is handled only by Stripe (PCI DSS).

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach creates a real risk of significant harm, we will notify you and the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada as the law requires, and we keep records of all breaches.

## 8. Quebec residents

If you live in Quebec, the *Act respecting the protection of personal information in the private sector* (Law 25) also applies. Our person in charge of the protection of personal information is our Privacy Officer, named above. We tell you when your information is used for a purpose you did not agree to, we assess the privacy impact before we transfer personal information outside Quebec, we keep a register of confidentiality incidents and report serious ones to the Commission d'accès à l'information, and our default settings are the most private (marketing is off until you opt in). You can ask for your information in a structured, commonly used format. The phone assistant drafts an order from a transcript; a person at the shop reviews and confirms it, and you can ask us to explain how it was drafted. You can complain to the Commission d'accès à l'information. A French version of this policy is available at [/legal/privacy?lang=fr](/legal/privacy?lang=fr).

## 9. Commercial messages (CASL)

Under Canada's Anti-Spam Legislation, we and the shops send promotional email, text, WhatsApp or push messages only with your consent, or where the law lets us rely on an existing business relationship. Consent is **separate for each shop and for Local Loyalty**, is never pre-ticked, and every promotional message names who is sending it, gives their contact details and has a free unsubscribe (STOP for text and WhatsApp). We keep a record of when and how you consented. Reminders and notices about something you ordered or booked are service messages and carry no advertising. When we message a shop owner about setting up their program, we identify ourselves and offer a way to stop those reminders.
