# Ads: barbershops and threading bars

Landing page: `/for-barbers`. Strategy: `docs/business/solo-pro-launch.md` §5.2. Voice: "If you're a barber, manage all your stuff in one place." Plain, short, no jargon.

**Audience.** Owners and chair renters at barbershops and threading bars in the GTA, 24 to 50. Interests: barbering, men's grooming, shop ownership. Also owners of two to six chairs, where per-seat pricing hurts most.

**Formats and link.** 9:16 first, 1:1 second, real product on a real phone in the Clear theme, no stock photography, no gradient backdrops, hook on screen in the first second. Link `/for-barbers?src=ad-<n>`; the page's sign-up carries `src=barbers`.

## Concepts

| # | Hook | Visual | CTA |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | No per-chair charge. Ever. | "$29.99 + $20 per barber" struck through; "$39 for the whole shop" | See the plan |
| 2 | How long's the wait? | The door tablet and a client's phone: "You're 3rd · about 40 min · we'll let you know" | Walk-in line, built in |
| 3 | Dad books, kids' cuts on the same card. | The family card screen | Family under one card |
| 4 | Chair renters keep their own clients. | The payouts page with a renter's private view | Payouts and chair rent, no spreadsheet |
| 5 | Back-to-school cuts, sent to every regular. | The promo template and the week's calendar filling | 4 promos a month |

The $39 on screen is read from the plan price in `plans.ts` when the creative is made. The Booksy figures come from the comparison table on the page (read Oct 28, 2026; re-check before running).

## Copy

**9:16.** Hook, three short beats, CTA. Line: "One price for the whole shop, however many chairs."

**1:1.** Primary text: "Bookings, the walk-in line, family cards and chair renters. One price for the whole shop. Free to start, prices in CAD." Headline: the hook. Button: Learn more.

## Budget

About 40% of the media budget in the first eight weeks ($600 a month of the $1,500 media share), the rest on nail techs. Concepts 1 and 2 get 60% until a winner shows. Same kill rule: stop above $90 per free signup for four weeks in a row; target $60 or less.
