# Ads: pet groomers (wave 2)

Landing page: `/for-groomers`. Strategy: `docs/business/solo-pro-launch.md` §5.2. Wave 2: run after the nail-tech and barber campaigns have a read at week 4. Voice: "If you groom dogs, check the vaccines before they walk in."

**Audience.** Independent and mobile groomers in the GTA, 25 to 50. Interests: dog grooming, pet care business. Exclude large chains.

**Formats and link.** 9:16 first, 1:1 second, real product in the Clear theme, no stock photography or gradient backdrops, hook in the first second. Link `/for-groomers?src=ad-<n>&v=<variant>` (`control` for concepts 1 to 3; `dms` and `noshows` exist on the page too, `docs/marketing/value-props.md` §2.2); the sign-up carries `src=groomers`. Deposits are optional and never a hook; the plan is Pro.

## Concepts

| # | Hook | Visual | CTA |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Vaccines checked before the groom. | A pet profile with the expiry date and the certificate photo | Free groom booking |
| 2 | Max is due. One tap to book. | The groom schedule reminder and the rebook link | Rebooking that runs itself |
| 3 | Prices by dog size, no back and forth. | The booking page showing only large-dog prices after picking Max | Set up in three minutes |
| 4 | The table isn't empty at 11:30. | The reminder with the vaccine check going out the day before; the "Deposit: off" switch beside the long groom | Get your booking link, free (`v=noshows`, T3) |

## Copy

**9:16.** Hook, three short beats, CTA. Line: "Vaccine dates, prices by dog size, and a nudge to rebook. Nobody takes a cut. Free to start."

**1:1.** Primary text: "Vaccine dates on every pet's profile, prices by dog size, and rebooking that runs itself. Free to start, prices in CAD." Headline: the hook. Button: Learn more.

## Budget

Starts at week 5 from the same pool, about $300 a month, taken from the winning nail-tech or barber concepts' share. Same kill rule and target as the other pages ($60 target, stop above $90 for four weeks).
