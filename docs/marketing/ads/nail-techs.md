# Ads: nail, brow and lash techs

Landing page: `/for-nail-techs` (`?trade=brows`, `?trade=lashes`, `?trade=makeup` swap the hero and the sample services). Strategy: `docs/business/solo-pro-launch.md` §5.2. Voice: the founder's, plain and a bit blunt. "If you're a nail tech, put your bookings, your clients and your reminders in one place." Canadian spelling, sentence case on screen text, no "Upgrade". **Oct 29:** deposits are optional and never the hook; the lead is "your clients stay yours" (`docs/marketing/value-props.md`), and each concept names the landing variant it pairs with (`?v=`).

**Audience.** Home-based and rented-chair nail, brow and lash techs in Toronto, Peel, York and Hamilton, 22 to 45, Instagram and Reels heavy. Interests: nail art, lash extensions, brow lamination, beauty pros. Exclude salon owners with more than five staff. Lookalike from the first 50 signups once there are 50.

**Formats.** 9:16 Reels and Stories first, 1:1 feed second. Every creative shows the real product on a real phone in the Clear theme (flat colours, no gradient backdrop, no stock photography). The hook is on screen in the first second. Captions are burned in: most people watch muted.

**Link and source.** Every ad links to `/for-nail-techs?src=ad-<n>&v=<variant>` (n is the concept number; the variant is the one in the table, or none for concepts 5 and 6, which take whatever the visitor's cookie says). The page's own sign-up carries `src=nail-techs`; the ad number is kept in the URL for the ads manager. During a page test (T1, T2 in `value-props.md` §3.5) one neutral ad links to the bare URL and the page splits visitors; the paired ads below are for the message test that follows.

## Copy

**9:16 (Reels and Stories).** On-screen text: the hook, then three beats of two to four words each, then the CTA. Spoken or captioned line: "If you're a nail tech, put your bookings, your clients and your reminders in one place. Nobody takes a cut. Free to start."

**1:1 (feed).** Primary text: "Instagram DMs, emails, a calendar link you sent once. Put it all in one place instead. A booking link with reminders, free to start. No marketplace, no cut of your clients. Prices in CAD." Headline: the hook. Button: Learn more.

**Do not say:** anything about licences or inspections; "no fees" (deposits, when used, carry 1% plus card processing; say "no cut of your clients" or "no new-client fee"); "Upgrade"; a deposit as the hook. Show only the sample pro on screen, never a real client.

## Budget

From §5.2 and §5.4: $2,000 a month for eight weeks across the nail-tech and barber campaigns, $500 of it on creative. Split for this vertical: about 60% of the media budget ($900 a month) on nail-tech concepts, since it is the lead wedge. Within it: during T1 one neutral ad carries the whole nail budget to the bare URL and the page splits (`value-props.md` §3.5); after T1 decides, concepts 1 and 2 as paired ads get 70% and concepts 3, 4 and 7 get 30% until a winner shows by cost per live booking link. Kill rule: cost per free signup above $90 for four weeks in a row stops the campaign and keeps the page for organic. Target: $60 or less.
