# Ads: nail, brow and lash techs

Landing page: `/for-nail-techs` (`?trade=brows`, `?trade=lashes`, `?trade=makeup` swap the hero and the sample services). Strategy: `docs/business/solo-pro-launch.md` §5.2. Voice: the founder's, plain and a bit blunt. "If you're a nail tech, stop booking in your DMs." Canadian spelling, sentence case on screen text, no "Upgrade".

**Audience.** Home-based and rented-chair nail, brow and lash techs in Toronto, Peel, York and Hamilton, 22 to 45, Instagram and Reels heavy. Interests: nail art, lash extensions, brow lamination, beauty pros. Exclude salon owners with more than five staff. Lookalike from the first 50 signups once there are 50.

**Formats.** 9:16 Reels and Stories first, 1:1 feed second. Every creative shows the real product on a real phone in the Clear theme (flat colours, no gradient backdrop, no stock photography). The hook is on screen in the first second. Captions are burned in: most people watch muted.

**Link and source.** Every ad links to `/for-nail-techs?src=ad-<n>` (n is the concept number). The page's own sign-up carries `src=nail-techs`; the ad number is kept in the URL for the ads manager.

## Copy

**9:16 (Reels and Stories).** On-screen text: the hook, then three beats of two to four words each, then the CTA. Spoken or captioned line: "If you're a nail tech, put your bookings, deposits and regulars in one place. Free to start."

**1:1 (feed).** Primary text: "Instagram DMs, emails, a calendar link you sent once. Put it all in one place instead. A booking link with deposits and reminders, free to start. Prices in CAD." Headline: the hook. Button: Learn more.

**Do not say:** anything about licences or inspections; "no fees" (deposits carry 1% plus card processing); "Upgrade". Show only the sample pro on screen, never a real client.

## Budget

From §5.2 and §5.4: $2,000 a month for eight weeks across the nail-tech and barber campaigns, $500 of it on creative. Split for this vertical: about 60% of the media budget ($900 a month) on nail-tech concepts, since it is the lead wedge. Within it: concepts 1, 2 and 4 get 70% in the first two weeks; concepts 3, 5 and 6 get 30% until a winner shows by cost per free signup. Kill rule: cost per free signup above $90 for four weeks in a row stops the campaign and keeps the page for organic. Target: $60 or less.
