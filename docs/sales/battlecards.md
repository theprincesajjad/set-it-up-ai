## SoPoints

**What it is:** Toronto company. One card across local GTA independents; each shop keeps its own points. Uses field agents. Same thesis as ours, same city. **Threat: high.**

**Pricing (Oct 21, 2026, PRD; ⚠ verify at sopoints.com/pricing):** free plan with a 5% transaction fee; paid plans US$49–199/month. Confirm what the 5% is charged on.

**Their strengths (be honest):** first mover in Toronto, the same network idea, people on the ground, merchants already trained on it.

**Our angle:**
- **0% fee** vs a percentage fee on their free plan.
- **Done for you**, including free migration of members and balances.
- **Density:** we launch whole streets at once with the BIA, so the network is real in week 1.
- **Later:** repair/auto workflows (Pro), which SoPoints doesn't offer as far as we know ⚠ verify.

**Questions to ask:** "What does SoPoints cost you in a typical month?" "How many of your customers use it?" "Did someone set it up for you?"

**Don't say:** anything about their finances, reliability or future. Never imply we're affiliated.

Full script: [scripts/sopoints-switch.md](scripts/sopoints-switch.md).

## Square Loyalty

**What it is:** Square's own loyalty add-on, built into the Square POS. Single merchant. **Threat: medium** (owns the counter for Square shops).

**Pricing (Oct 21, 2026, PRD; ⚠ verify at square.com/ca/en/software/loyalty):** from about $60/month per location (CAD). Square has used tiered pricing; check the current tiers and the merchant's actual bill.

**Their strengths:** native to the POS, enrolment on the customer display, automatic text notifications, no integration needed, trusted brand.

**Our angle:**
- **Keep Square.** We connect to it and auto-award (confirm the connector is live).
- **$0, any number of locations**, vs per-location pricing.
- **The network:** customers find you through other shops. Square can't offer that.
- Birthday, referrals, VIP levels, double-points promos and email are all free with us (some Square marketing tools are a separate subscription ⚠ verify).

**Where Square wins:** texting customers is included in Square Loyalty; our Free plan has no SMS (comes with Plus later). Say so if asked.

Full script: [scripts/square-loyalty-users.md](scripts/square-loyalty-users.md).

## Clover Rewards (and Clover App Market loyalty apps)

**What it is:** Clover (Fiserv) POS has its own rewards options and third-party loyalty apps in the Clover App Market. ⚠ **Verify** what's available in Canada today: features, pricing and availability vary by Clover plan and by the bank or reseller the merchant bought Clover from.

**Their strengths:** inside the POS the merchant already uses; may be bundled in their plan.

**Our angle:**
- Keep Clover; we auto-award from Clover sales (confirm live).
- If they pay for a loyalty app from the App Market, we're $0.
- The shared street network, wallet pass, and done-for-you setup.

**Questions to ask:** "Is loyalty part of your Clover plan, or a separate app? What does it cost? Do your customers use it?"

## Wallet stamp cards (Loopy Loyalty, Stamp Me, CHCKN, BonusQR-type)

**What they are:** digital stamp cards that live in Apple/Google Wallet. Customers scan a QR; no app. **Threat: medium** (customers love no-download).

**Pricing (PRD, citing CHCKN's 2026 guide; ⚠ verify each vendor):** free to about $50/month.

**Their strengths:** no download, simple, cheap, quick to set up.

**Our angle:**
- **Same no-download join** (Apple/Google Wallet pass), and free.
- **The network:** their card sits alone in the wallet; ours shows every shop on the street.
- **More program types:** points per dollar, per visit, items, rewards catalogue, VIP levels, birthday, referrals, promos.
- **POS auto-award** and staff logins with an audit trail (stops stamp abuse).
- **Done for you.**

## TapMango

**What it is:** Canadian loyalty and marketing platform for single merchants and chains; branded apps, tablets, marketing tools. **Threat: low–medium.** ⚠ verify current product lineup.

**Pricing (PRD; ⚠ verify):** roughly $59–299/month or custom quote.

**Their strengths:** mature product, branded app options, strong marketing automation, good for chains that want their own brand.

**Our angle:** $0 vs a monthly fee; no merchant app to promote; shared neighbourhood network; done-for-you setup; unlimited locations free.

**Where they win:** a chain that wants **its own branded app**. That's not us. Say so.

## Kangaroo Rewards

**What it is:** Montreal-based loyalty platform for small and multi-location businesses; POS integrations, branded options. **Threat: low–medium.** ⚠ verify.

**Pricing (PRD; ⚠ verify):** monthly tiers, roughly in the same range as TapMango (~$59–299/month) or custom.

**Our angle:** same as TapMango: $0, no merchant-branded app to promote, the local network, done for you, unlimited locations.

## Ritual

**What it is:** Toronto-founded order-ahead app for restaurants and cafés. Commission per order. **Threat: low** for loyalty (PRD notes reviews suggesting fewer restaurants use it now ⚠ verify).

**Our angle (today):** we're loyalty, not a marketplace. We don't compete with order-ahead yet. Keep Ritual if it works for them.
**Our angle (later, Plus):** order-ahead and pickup at a flat monthly price plus payment fees, instead of a per-order marketplace commission. **Don't pitch this until Plus is live.**

## Paper punch cards

**What it is:** the most common "program" on any strip. **Threat: it's the status quo.**

**Their strengths:** free, simple, customers understand it, no tech.

**Our angle:**
- Same idea, can't be lost, can't be faked (audit trail).
- You finally know who your regulars are, and can tell them about promos (with consent).
- The network brings new people in. Paper never does.
- **We honour every old paper card** on switch day.

**Best line:** "You already believe in loyalty, or you wouldn't have these cards. We just make them work harder, for free."

## National coalitions (Scene+, PC Optimum, Triangle, Air Miles)

**What they are:** big national programs. Scene+ has 15M+ members, and Shell moved from Air Miles to Scene+ in 2026 (PRD). PC Optimum covers Loblaw stores and Shoppers Drug Mart. Triangle (Canadian Tire) is linking with RBC Avion. **Not open to independents.** **Threat: indirect** (they shape customer expectations).

**Our angle:**
- **"Your street's Scene+."** Independents finally get a shared program too.
- Small, frequent, local rewards: a free coffee, not a flight. (60% of Canadians prefer small, frequent rewards; Canadians hold ~15 programs and actively use far fewer; see PRD.)
- **Buy local:** 42% of Canadians report using Canadian programs more (LoyalT 2025, PRD).

**Don't say:** we're "like Scene+" in a way that suggests a partnership. We're not affiliated with any coalition.
