# Switching from SoPoints

Last updated: Oct 21, 2026 · **Competitor facts last checked: Oct 21, 2026 (from the PRD's research). Re-verify on [sopoints.com/pricing](https://sopoints.com/pricing) before every use.**

SoPoints is a Toronto company with the same basic idea we have: one card across local shops, each shop keeps its own points, and field agents. They're a real, credible competitor. **Be respectful.** The merchant chose them for good reasons; we're offering a better deal, not saying they made a mistake.

## Before you pitch: re-verify

1. Open SoPoints' pricing page on your phone. Screenshot it with the date visible.
2. Check: Is the free plan still listed with a transaction fee? What percentage? **What exactly is the fee charged on** (all sales, sales by members, redeemed rewards)? What do the paid plans cost, and in which currency?
3. If anything differs from this page, **use what the page says today** and tell your lead so we can update this file.

If you can't verify, don't quote numbers. Say: "Check what you're paying them per month and we'll compare."

## Worked example (illustrative only)

Only if the 5% applies to the sales it's charged on, and the merchant agrees with the numbers:

> A café does $3,000/month in sales that the fee applies to. At 5%, that's **$150/month**, or **$1,800/year**. With us, it's $0.

Always use **their** numbers. Ask: "Do you know roughly what SoPoints costs you a month?" Or: "Can we look at your last statement together?"

## The pitch

> "SoPoints had the right idea, and a lot of good shops use it. We do the same thing with two differences: **no fee on loyalty at all**, and **we set it up and move your members for you, free**. And we're building the street block by block, so [Anchor shops] are already on here."

## Objections

**"I'm happy with SoPoints."**
> "That's great to hear. If the fee ever bothers you, we're here. Can I leave this so you can compare?" Log `contacted`, current loyalty "SoPoints", revisit after launch week.

**"Switching is a hassle."**
> "That's why we do it. We import your members and their balances, set up your program to match, and train your staff. Your customers keep their points."

**"My customers have SoPoints cards."**
> "They'll join here with the same phone number, and we carry their balance over. For launch week, give them a bonus for switching."

**"I'm in a contract."**
> "Let's check your terms together. If there's a notice period, we can set everything up to switch on the day it ends."

**"What's the catch with you?"**
> "Loyalty is free forever. Later we'll offer optional tools like order-ahead or repair approvals. That's how we make money, and you never have to buy them."

## The migration offer (free)

1. **Export:** ask the merchant to export their member list and balances from SoPoints, if SoPoints allows it. The merchant owns that relationship; check SoPoints' terms first. If export isn't possible, we honour balances at the counter: customers show their old balance and staff add it once (logged as a manual adjustment).
2. **Import:** we use the product's import tool (`customers.csv_import`).
3. **Consent:** imported customers keep their points, but **we don't email them** unless the merchant has a record of express consent that covers it. Members confirm their own marketing preferences when they first scan or join.
4. **Match the program:** same reward thresholds if possible, so customers notice nothing but the better experience.
5. **Switch day:** counter sign "We've moved our rewards. Same points, now on Local Loyalty," plus double points that week.
6. **Cancel SoPoints:** the merchant cancels their own account per SoPoints' terms. We never contact SoPoints on their behalf.

## Rules

- Never call SoPoints names or suggest they're unreliable. Facts only, with dates.
- Never imply SoPoints is "going away" or that we're affiliated.
- Reps don't pose as merchants or customers to gather intel. Competitive research (trying SoPoints as a customer, visiting their shops, the PRD's test-merchant task) is founder-led and must respect SoPoints' terms of service.
- Tag the lead's `currentLoyalty` as "SoPoints" so we can track switch rates.
