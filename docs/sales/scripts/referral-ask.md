# Referral Ask: "Who's next door?"

Last updated: Oct 21, 2026

A referral from a happy merchant on the same street is our best lead. The network gets more valuable to them with every shop that joins, so asking isn't a favour; it's in their interest.

## When to ask

1. **End of the setup visit** (minute 19), when the first member just joined.
2. **Day 7 call**, if they're at 25+ members.
3. **Day 30 call**, always.

## The ask

> "Every shop that joins makes your program more useful, because their customers see you on the map. Who on the street do you think would like this? Anyone you're friendly with?"

Then make it easy:

> "Would you mind if I said you sent me? Or even better, could you text them now and say I'm coming by?"

**If they name someone:** get the name of the shop and the owner, and ask what the owner is like ("When are they usually in?"). Log the new lead with "Referred by [Shop]" in notes.

**If they text or walk you over:** that's gold. Go now if it's a quiet time.

## The text they can send (offer to type it for them)

> "Hey [Name], I just set up Local Loyalty for [Shop]. It's free, no fees, and they set it up for you. [Rep name] is on the street today; worth 20 minutes."

## What we give back

- Their shop gets featured in the strip's launch-week post (if they want).
- A thank-you: they're named in the BIA visit report as a founding shop.
- We **don't** pay merchants cash for referrals in phase 1. Keep it neighbourly.

## Logging

Every referral = a new lead with "Referred by [Shop]" in notes. Track referred leads separately in the weekly review: they should close faster.
