# Student Rep Program

Last updated: Oct 21, 2026 · Owner: founder / head of sales

Part-time student reps do the street work: mapping, calls, walk-ins, 20-minute setups and check-ins. This page covers hiring them legally, training, the daily routine, targets, pay and CRM hygiene.

> **Not legal advice.** The founder should book the one-hour employment lawyer call from the PRD's 30-day list and have the offer letter and bonus terms reviewed before the first hire.

---

## 1. Hiring legally in Ontario

**The rule:** reps are **paid employees**. Not commission-only interns, not unpaid "ambassadors".

| Option | Use it? | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Part-time hourly employees + per-activation bonus** | **Yes, default** | Clear under the Employment Standards Act (ESA); predictable cost |
| **Co-op placements** through a college/university program | Yes, as a pipeline | Unpaid placements are only legal when they're part of an approved school program. We recommend paying co-op students anyway: better reps, less risk |
| Commission-only interns | **No** | The ESA treats most interns as employees owed minimum wage (PRD, McMillan) |
| Independent contractors | Not for now | Misclassification risk; only with a lawyer-reviewed agreement and genuinely independent work |

### ESA basics to get right (confirm with the lawyer and payroll provider)

- **Minimum wage: $17.95/hour from Oct 1, 2026** (general rate). We pay the general rate to everyone, including students under 18 (who could legally get the lower student rate). Recommend hiring 18+.
- **Pay every hour worked:** training, team huddles, time travelling **between** shops during a shift, and time spent logging in the CRM.
- **Vacation pay:** at least 4% of wages (including bonuses).
- **Public holiday pay** per the ESA.
- **Eating period:** a 30-minute (unpaid) break no later than 5 hours into a shift.
- **The three-hour rule:** if a rep who regularly works more than 3 hours is sent home early, they're owed at least 3 hours' pay. Don't cancel shifts at the last minute.
- **Written offer** with pay rate, bonus terms, and hours. Pay stubs, records, deductions (CPP, EI, income tax), T4s.
- **WSIB:** check whether registration is required for our business classification.
- **Health and safety:** reps work on the street, alone. Have a written workplace violence and harassment policy, a check-in buddy system, and the rule "if a situation feels wrong, leave."
- **Job postings:** include the pay rate and bonus. (Ontario's pay-transparency rules for postings apply to larger employers, but it's good practice for us anyway.)
- **Bonuses must be earned on objective rules** written in advance (below), and paid on the next payroll.

### Where to find reps

- Campus job boards and co-op offices: TMU (Toronto Metropolitan University), U of T, York, George Brown, Humber, Centennial, OCAD.
- Students who live near the strip (they know the shops; shorter commute).
- Language skills matter: many owners speak Portuguese, Polish, Greek, Cantonese, Mandarin, Korean, Tamil, Punjabi, Tagalog, Spanish, Ukrainian and more. List languages in the posting.
- Hire for: warmth, reliability, comfort talking to strangers. Not for "sales experience".

**Interview test (10 minutes):** the candidate walks into a pretend café and gives the 60-second door script after reading it once. Watch for: smile, listening, recovering from "I'm busy."

## 2. Training plan (3 paid days)

**Before day 1 (paid, 2 hours at home):** read [README.md](README.md), [scripts/walk-in.md](scripts/walk-in.md), and two playbooks (cafés and one service niche). Install the merchant app and log into the demo merchant.

### Day 1 — Shadow
- 1 hour: product walkthrough with the lead. Join as a customer, award, redeem, network map, dashboard, rep console (create a lead, move stages, create a merchant from a template, print the QR kit).
- 1 hour: role-play the door script and the 90-second demo 5 times each.
- 4 hours: **shadow** the lead rep on the strip. Watch 10+ conversations and at least one 20-minute setup. Take notes on what worked.
- Debrief: 3 things you'll copy, 1 thing you'd do differently.

### Day 2 — Co-pilot
- Rep leads the door script; lead rep steps in only if needed.
- Rep runs at least one full 20-minute setup with the lead watching.
- Rep logs every activity in the console; lead reviews the entries at the end.
- Covers: pharmacy and alcohol no-earn rules, CASL basics, what's not live yet (POS connectors, Plus/Pro).

### Day 3 — Solo
- Rep works a section of the strip alone; lead is reachable by phone and nearby.
- End-of-day review: activities logged, setups done, stage accuracy.

### Sign-off checklist (lead signs before rep works unsupervised)
- [ ] Gives the 60-second door script naturally
- [ ] Runs the 90-second demo without notes
- [ ] Creates a merchant from a template and checks the no-earn categories
- [ ] Knows the pharmacy, alcohol, tobacco/vape/lottery rules
- [ ] Knows which POS connectors are live this week and doesn't promise others
- [ ] Never quotes a competitor price without checking it today
- [ ] Logs every activity same day; every open lead has a next action date

## 3. Daily routine (6-hour shift, e.g. 10:00–16:30 with a 30-min break)

| Time | Activity |
| --- | --- |
| 10:00–10:15 | Huddle (in person or call): today's section, booked visits, day 7/30 calls due |
| 10:15–11:45 | **Service shops & retail** (quiet mornings): walk-ins, booked setups. Day 7/30 calls between visits |
| 11:45–12:15 | Log activities. Update stages. |
| 12:15–12:45 | Break (unpaid) |
| 12:45–14:00 | **Calls** to book visits (food shops are busy at lunch; call services and retail) |
| 14:00–16:00 | **Cafés, bakeries, restaurants, bars** (their quiet window): walk-ins and setups |
| 16:00–16:30 | Log everything, set next actions, message the lead with the day's numbers |

Adjust to the strip: the niche playbooks list best hours. Never pitch during a rush.

## 4. Activity targets

**Definitions** (use these words, they matter for pay):
- **Setup** = merchant signed **and** live after a 20-minute visit (program set, QR stand placed, one staff trained, first member joined). The PRD calls these "activations per shift".
- **Activated** = the product marks the merchant activated: **25 members with at least one visit**.

| Per 6-hour shift (after week 1) | Target |
| --- | --- |
| Conversations with owners/managers (door + phone) | 12–15 |
| 90-second demos | 5 |
| **Setups** | **~3** |
| Day 7 / day 30 check-ins | 2–4 |
| Activities logged same day | 100% |

| Per rep per week (3 shifts) | Target |
| --- | --- |
| Setups | 8–9 |
| Setups that activate within 45 days | 60%+ |

Expect fewer setups in the first week of a strip (mapping) and more in week 2.

## 5. Comp plan (aligned to the ~$90 CAC budget)

| Component | Amount | Rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hourly wage | **$17.95/hr** minimum (recommend $18.50 for retention; see budget note) | All hours worked, including training and logging |
| **Activation bonus** | **$25 per merchant** | Paid when a merchant the rep set up is activated (25 members with a visit) within 45 days of setup. One rep per merchant; if two reps worked it, the setup rep gets it |
| Strip gate bonus | $50 per rep | Paid when the strip passes the 60% activation gate |

### Cost per merchant (at 3 setups per 6-hour shift)

| Line | Cost | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Wages: 6 h × $17.95 ÷ 3 setups | $35.90 | ~2 paid hours per merchant all-in |
| Employer costs on wages (~13%: vacation pay, CPP, EI, WSIB if applicable) | ~$4.70 | Confirm the % with the payroll provider |
| Activation bonus incl. ~13% load | ~$28.25 | Only paid on activation |
| Counter kit (QR stand, signs, stuffers) | $15.00 | PRD |
| Strip gate bonus share (3 reps × $50 ÷ 35 merchants, loaded) | ~$4.85 | |
| **Total** | **~$88.70** | Inside the PRD's ~$90 budget, assuming every merchant activates |

The activation bonus is only paid on merchants that activate. At the 60% gate, the expected bonus cost is ~$17, so the realistic blended cost is closer to **~$77 per signed merchant**. The table shows the worst case.

**Watch-outs:** at $18.50/hr the worst-case total is about $89.90, right at the line. If reps average only 2 setups per shift, wages plus load per merchant jump to ~$61 and the worst-case total to ~$109, over budget. **The number to manage is setups per shift**, which is why mapping, booking visits in advance and quiet-hour timing matter.

This replaces the PRD's "$50 rep commission" line with hourly wages, which is the legal route the PRD itself recommends.

## 6. CRM hygiene (rep console)

The rep console tracks leads through six stages. **If it isn't in the console, it didn't happen.** Until the console is fully live, use the shared sheet with exactly the same columns and stages so it can be imported later.

### Stages

| Stage | Means | Move here when | Required before moving |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `new` | Mapped, not yet contacted | Entered on the mapping walk | Name, vertical, address |
| `contacted` | We've spoken or emailed | First call, email or door conversation | Contact name & role, `currentLoyalty`, outcome note |
| `visit_booked` | A setup visit is scheduled | They agree to a time | `nextActionAt` = visit time; phone number |
| `signed` | Owner said yes and the merchant exists | Merchant created in the console | Linked merchant; day 7 call set in `nextActionAt` |
| `activated` | 25 members with a visit | Product marks the merchant activated | Nothing; confirm it matches the merchant |
| `lost` | Not now | Clear no, DNC, unsubscribe, closed, out of scope | **Lost reason** (below) |

### Rules

1. **Log the same day.** Every call, email, visit and note is an activity (`call`, `email`, `visit`, `note`) with an outcome.
2. **Every open lead has a `nextActionAt`.** Only `activated` and `lost` leads may have none.
3. **Fill `currentLoyalty`** with what they use (e.g. "paper punch card", "SoPoints", "Square Loyalty", "none"). It drives the battlecard and our competitive reporting.
4. **Lost reasons** (pick one, then add detail): `not_interested`, `has_competitor` (name it), `banner_or_franchise_rules`, `closed_or_moving`, `out_of_scope`, `dnc_or_unsubscribe`, `no_decision_maker_reachable`.
5. **DNC and unsubscribe are permanent.** Mark it in notes, never contact again by that channel.
6. **No duplicates.** Search before creating. One lead per storefront.
7. **Flag upsell signals** in notes with the exact tag "Plus candidate" or "Pro candidate" and one line on why.
8. **Flag multi-location** with "multi-location" and hand to the founder within 24 hours.
9. **No personal data you don't need.** Business contact info only. Never store customer data in lead notes.

### Weekly review (Friday, 30 minutes, paid)
- Pipeline by stage per rep; setups per shift; activations.
- Leads with no next action (should be zero).
- Lost reasons: any patterns?
- Top objection of the week and the best answer someone found. Update the playbook.

## 7. Rep code of conduct

- Be a good neighbour. Buy a coffee. Leave when asked.
- Never pitch during a rush or to customers.
- Never promise features, dates or prices that aren't live.
- Never badmouth competitors. Facts, dated.
- Never change a no-earn category (prescriptions, alcohol, tobacco, vapes, lottery) to close a deal.
- Never ask for a merchant's passwords. Owners log into their own POS.
- Report any problem (an upset merchant, a safety issue, a product bug) to the lead the same day.
