# Customer Acquisition (and the Later Upsell Path)

Last updated: Oct 21, 2026

**The counter is the channel.** We don't buy ads to find shoppers. Every shop that joins signs up its own regulars, and the network shows those regulars every other shop on the street. Near-zero cost, if staff actually ask.

The product goal: a customer joins at the counter in **under 30 seconds, without downloading an app**.

---

## 1. The staff counter script

Teach it at the setup visit. Leave it on a card at the till. Repeat it on the day 7 call.

**The ask (at payment):**
> "Do you have points with us? It's free, just your phone number."

**If yes:** type the number, award, done. "You've got [X] stamps. It's double this week!"

**If they hesitate:**
> "No app, no emails unless you want them. It's on your phone's wallet, and it works at [Café] and [Bakery] down the street too."

**If no:** "No problem!" Never push twice.

**Launch week add-on:**
> "It's launch week on [Street], so it's double points everywhere."

**Rules for staff:**
- Ask **every** customer for the first two weeks. That's the whole trick.
- Ask at payment, not when they walk in.
- Never sign someone up without asking them. Never type in a phone number a customer didn't give for this.
- Marketing emails only go to people who tick the opt-in. Staff don't promise or imply otherwise.

## 2. Launch-week double points

- Every shop on the strip runs **double points / double stamps** in the same week (free feature: bonus point promos).
- Signage says the same thing everywhere: **"Double points on [Street] this week."** Repetition across shops is what makes customers notice.
- The network perk runs alongside: bonus points when a customer visits their **3rd shop** on the strip.
- In week 4, Passport Week with the BIA (visit 5 shops, enter the draw).

## 3. QR stand placement

| Do | Don't |
| --- | --- |
| At the till, at eye level, facing the customer | Behind the register or under the counter |
| Next to the payment terminal or tip screen | By the door (people are leaving) |
| A second stand at the pickup counter in cafés | On a cluttered counter full of other signs |
| Table tents in restaurants and bars | Covered by the tip jar |
| Mirror sticker at each barber/salon station | Somewhere staff have to move it to work |

Check it on every walk of the strip. A hidden stand is the #1 reason a shop stalls under 10 members.

## 4. Receipt and bag stuffers

- **Bag stuffer card** (business-card size): "You're one of our regulars. Get stamps for it: scan to join, no app." Shop logo + QR.
- **Receipt message:** if the POS allows a custom receipt footer, add "Ask about points: free, just your phone number."
- **Coasters** in bars and cafés with the QR code.
- **Window decal:** "Earn points here · Local Loyalty · [Street]". Every shop, same design, so the street looks connected.
- **Order bag sticker** for takeout.

## 5. Community channels

| Channel | How | Etiquette |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **BIA channels** (newsletter, Instagram, website) | We write it, they send it | Their voice; let them edit |
| **Neighbourhood Instagram accounts** (local food, "what's on" and community accounts) | Offer an exclusive: "first look at the Passport Week prize basket" | Ask; don't pay for posts without disclosing it as an ad |
| **Merchants' own Instagram** | Give every shop a ready-to-post story and caption for launch week | Their account, their choice |
| **Reddit (r/toronto, neighbourhood subs)** | Mostly let merchants and the BIA share. If we post, use a personal account that clearly says "I work for Local Loyalty", once, in an appropriate thread | Read each subreddit's rules first; many ban self-promotion. **Never** astroturf (fake "I found this cool app" posts), never use multiple accounts, never ask people to upvote |
| **Local press** (neighbourhood papers and newsletters, community blogs, city food and culture outlets, local radio and TV community segments) | Pitch the human story: "30 shops on Roncesvalles band together against the big chains' loyalty programs," with a merchant quote and the BIA | Short email to a named reporter, 5 lines, photos attached, one follow-up |
| **Community boards** (library, community centre, school newsletters) | Passport Week poster | Ask permission |

Keep a list of local outlets per strip in the launch folder; confirm they're still active before pitching.

## 6. Consent and privacy (customers)

- Joining = earning points. **Marketing messages are separate** and need opt-in (CASL). Customers can opt in per shop and **mute any shop**.
- Each merchant sees only its own customers. The BIA sees only anonymous totals.
- Points never expire by surprise and are always redeemable at the shop that issued them (lesson from Drop in the PRD).

## 7. The later upsell path to Plus / Pro

Plus ($39/mo) and Pro ($79/mo) aren't for sale yet. The product plan opens them once the network reaches roughly **150 active merchants**. Our job now is to **notice and log** the signals so the founder has a warm list on day one.

**How to log:** add "Plus candidate" or "Pro candidate" to the lead notes, plus one line ("chases repair approvals by phone ~10 times a day").

### Trigger signals by vertical

| Vertical | Signal you'll see | Plan | Feature that solves it |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Phone/computer repair | Staff on the phone chasing quote approvals; shelf of uncollected devices | **Pro** | Service tickets, quote approval in-app, deposits, "ready" alerts |
| Auto shops | Cars sitting in bays waiting for approval; paper invoices | **Pro** | Quote approval with e-signature log, deposits, invoices, drop-off scheduling |
| Alterations / dry cleaning | "Is it ready?" calls; uncollected garments | **Pro** | Tickets, pickup-ready alerts |
| Print shops | Quotes and proofs lost in email | **Pro** | Quote approvals, deposits, invoices |
| Salons, barbers, groomers | No-shows; asking for deposits | **Pro** | Deposits, drop-off/appointment reminders, staff permissions |
| Cafés | Morning line, customers walking out; paying commission for pickup orders | **Plus** | Order-ahead, pickup status, in-app payments |
| Bakeries | Holiday pre-order chaos; phone orders | **Plus** | Order-ahead with pickup slots |
| Restaurants, bars | Heavy delivery-app or phone pickup orders | **Plus** | Order-ahead, pickup, SMS for events |
| Retail, bookstores, florists | Holds and special orders by phone; texting VIPs by hand | **Plus** | Reserve-and-pickup, SMS, nearby push promos |
| Fitness | Churn worries; texting lapsed members | **Plus** | Automations ("We miss you"), SMS, retention reports |
| Grocery / pharmacy front store | Phone orders for pickup | **Plus** | Pickup ordering, SMS (never prescription-related) |

### When Plus/Pro open (founder-led)

1. Founder calls the "Pro candidate" list first. Repair and auto are the strongest (and the PRD's $100 refundable pilot deposit test).
2. Offer opted-in beta access to merchants who are already activated.
3. Never move a free feature to paid. Upsell only adds.
