# Local Loyalty Sales & Go-to-Market

Owner: Head of Sales & GTM · Last updated: Oct 21, 2026

This folder is the sales kit. A new part-time rep should be able to read this page, skim their niche playbook, and walk a strip tomorrow.

> **Where this differs from the PRD:** the founder's model wins. Loyalty is **free forever**: no transaction fee, unlimited locations, unlimited staff, and we set it up. Phase 1 is about building one large network on one app. Paid Plus/Pro add-ons come later, once a strip has density. Network ads and BIA campaigns come after that.

## The sales motion on one page

**We win one street at a time. Not one shop at a time, and never "all of Toronto".**

1. **Pick the strip.** Use the scorecard in [neighbourhood-launch-plan.md](neighbourhood-launch-plan.md). Target: 30–40 shops within a 10-minute walk, with a good mix of daily-visit shops (cafés, bakeries) and higher-ticket service shops (repair, auto, alterations).
2. **BIA first (week 0).** The founder pitches the BIA manager ([bia-partnership.md](bia-partnership.md)). We ask for three things: a newsletter intro, a member list, and permission to say "the BIA is supporting this." A warm intro roughly doubles how many owners will talk to us.
3. **Map the strip.** Walk it, list every storefront in the rep console as a lead (`new`), and tag its vertical and current loyalty method.
4. **Anchors first.** Sign 3–5 well-liked "anchor" shops (the busy café, the bakery with the line, the shop everyone knows). Every later pitch becomes "Bakery X and Café Y are already on it."
5. **Sweep the street (weeks 1–2).** Call, email, then walk in ([scripts/](scripts/)). Every yes gets a **20-minute setup visit** on the spot or booked within 48 hours.
6. **Seed members (week 3, launch week).** Street-wide **double points**. Every shop's staff asks every customer: "Can I get your phone number for points?"
7. **Passport week (week 4).** With the BIA: visit 5 shops, enter a draw for a prize basket donated by merchants. This is what makes the network real for customers.
8. **Check-ins.** Day 7 and day 30 calls ([scripts/follow-ups.md](scripts/follow-ups.md)). A merchant is **activated** at 25 members with at least one visit.
9. **Ask for the next door.** Every happy merchant gets the referral ask ([scripts/referral-ask.md](scripts/referral-ask.md)).
10. **Gate.** Do not start the next strip until **60% of signed shops are activated**. Fix onboarding first if we miss.

**Unit economics to protect:** about **$90 to acquire a merchant** (rep wages, activation bonus, counter kit). See [rep-program.md](rep-program.md).

## Rules of the road (read these twice)

1. **Never promise something that isn't live.** Before your first shift, ask your lead which POS connectors are live *this week*. If the shop's POS isn't connected yet, the honest answer is "staff type the phone number, it takes 5 seconds, and we'll switch on auto-award when your POS is connected."
2. **Plus/Pro are "later, optional".** Never quote a launch date. Never say a free feature will become paid. It won't.
3. **Competitor facts are dated.** Every competitor price in this kit has a "checked" date. Re-check the competitor's own pricing page before you quote it to a merchant, and say where it came from.
4. **Be respectful about competitors.** Merchants chose those tools. "Here's how we're different" beats "they're bad" every time.
5. **Pharmacies: front store only.** No points or discounts on prescriptions or pharmacy services. The template enforces this; you never override it.
6. **Alcohol, tobacco, vapes, lottery: no-earn.** The templates keep these out of earning and rewards. Don't change that to win a deal.
7. **CASL.** Merchants can only email or text customers who opted in. Our own cold emails always identify us and include an unsubscribe link.
8. **Log everything** in the rep console the same day. If it isn't in the console, it didn't happen.
