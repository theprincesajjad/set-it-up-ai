# Playbook: Alterations & Dry Cleaning

Templates: `alterations` (tailor) and `dry_cleaning` · Wave 4 (future payer) · Later plan: **Pro** · Last updated: Oct 21, 2026

**Why they matter:** regular weekly or monthly customers, owner always on site, decides fast. Strong Pro fit later: tickets, pickup-ready alerts, uncollected garments.

## Who to talk to & when

- **Decision maker:** owner, almost always behind the counter. Often family-run; English may be a second language. Have the one-pager and be patient.
- **Best time:** **10:00 am–12:00 pm** or **1:30–3:30 pm** weekdays.
- **Never:** 7:30–9:30 am and 5–7 pm (commuter drop-off and pickup), Saturdays.

## Their world & pains

- Paper tickets; customers lose them.
- Uncollected garments pile up; calling customers takes time.
- "Is it ready yet?" calls all day.
- Price pressure and fewer people wearing suits since remote work.

## Current tools they use

- POS: paper ticket books, cash register, SPOT, CleanCloud, Square.
- Loyalty: "10th shirt free" cards, nothing.

## Pitch angle

> "Your regulars come every week. This gives them a free shirt press after a few visits, it can't be lost like a paper card, and it shows your shop to everyone on the street. Free."

## 90-second demo

1. Scan the demo QR; welcome 25 points.
2. Staff: phone number, $38 drop-off → 38 points.
3. Reward: Free shirt press at 100 (dry cleaning) / $10 off at 300 (alterations).
4. Promo: "Winter coat cleaning week: double points."
5. Network map.

## Objections & answers

**"I already use paper punch cards."** "Keep the deal; we'll move the cards."
**"I already use SoPoints / Square Loyalty / Stamp Me."** "No fee, free migration." See switch scripts.
**"I don't want another tablet."** "Your phone is enough."
**"My customers won't download an app."** "Phone number at drop-off. That's it."
**"What's the catch?"** "Free forever. Later there'll be optional Pro tools, like 'your order is ready' messages and tickets. Never required."
**"I'm not good with technology."** "That's what I'm here for. I set it up, show one person, and I call you in a week."

## Recommended program

| Setting | `dry_cleaning` default | `alterations` default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Earn type | 1 pt per $ | 1 pt per $ |
| Welcome bonus | 25 | 25 |
| Reward | Free shirt press at 100 | $10 off at 300 |

## Launch-week promo

- Double points all week.
- Counter sign in plain language; ask if they want a translated version (log the language; we'll make one).

## Future upsell (Pro)

Signals: wall of uncollected garments; "is it ready?" calls; paper tickets.
Pro pitch (later): digital tickets, "ready for pickup" alerts, quotes for big alterations (wedding dresses), deposits.
