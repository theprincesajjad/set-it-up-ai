# Playbook: Restaurants

Template: `restaurant` · Wave 3 (destination) · Later plan: Plus · Last updated: Oct 21, 2026

**Why they matter:** higher tickets, groups of diners, and evening traffic that keeps the strip alive after the cafés close. Restaurants were the one category above average on every index in the LoyalT 2025 study (see PRD). They're also prime Plus candidates later (order-ahead, pickup).

## Who to talk to & when

- **Decision maker:** owner. In bigger spots, the GM can often decide but the owner signs. Chef-owners are in the kitchen; front-of-house owners are easier to catch.
- **Best time:** **2:30–4:30 pm**, Tuesday to Thursday, between lunch and dinner service. Many open only at 5 pm; knock at 3:30 when prep has started.
- **Never:** during service (11:30–2 pm, 5:30–9 pm), Friday/Saturday evenings, Mondays (often closed or the owner's day off).
- **Tip:** use the back door only if invited. Ask the host for the owner's name and best day, and come back.

## Their world & pains

- Delivery apps take a large commission per order and own the customer relationship.
- Weeknight dining rooms are half empty; weekends are full.
- Staff turnover, rising food costs, thin margins.
- They don't know who their regulars are unless they remember faces.
- Reservation platforms charge fees and also own the guest data.

## Current tools they use

- POS: TouchBistro (popular in Toronto), Toast, Lightspeed Restaurant, Square for Restaurants, Clover.
- Reservations: OpenTable, Resy, Tock, or a phone and a book.
- Ordering: Uber Eats, DoorDash, SkipTheDishes, Ritual, their own website.
- Loyalty: often nothing, some POS-native loyalty, SoPoints, punch cards for lunch spots.

## Pitch angle

> "Your weekends are full. This fills Tuesday. It's free, it brings your regulars back with a reason, and it puts you in front of everyone who joined at the café and bakery on this street. No commission, ever, on loyalty."

## 90-second demo

1. Scan the demo restaurant QR. Welcome: 50 points.
2. Staff view: phone number + bill amount ($62) → 620 points. *"10 points per dollar. Feels generous, costs you little."*
3. Rewards catalogue: Free appetizer (250), $10 off (500).
4. Promo: "Tuesday double points." *"Use it to move your slow nights."*
5. Network map: other shops on the strip, and members who live nearby.

## Objections & answers

**"I already use paper punch cards."** (lunch spots)
> "Keep it simple: switch the template to stamps, '10 lunches, 11th free', and we'll transfer existing cards."

**"I already use SoPoints."**
> "On a $60 bill, a percentage fee isn't small. Ours is zero. We'll move your members." Re-verify their current pricing first.

**"I already use Square Loyalty / my POS has loyalty."**
> "If it's Square, we connect and you can drop the add-on fee. If it's another POS, staff type the phone number when they close the bill, or we auto-award from Toast, Lightspeed or Clover once connected." TouchBistro: confirm with product whether a connector exists; if not, phone-number entry.

**"I don't want another tablet."**
> "Use the server's phone or the host stand tablet you already have."

**"My customers won't download an app."**
> "No download. The server says 'want points on this?' and types the number, or the guest scans the QR on the bill folder."

**"What's the catch?"**
> "Loyalty is free forever. Later we'll offer optional order-ahead and pickup at a flat monthly price, with no per-order commission. That's for when you want it."

**"Discounts devalue my food."**
> "Rewards go to people who already spent, say, $250 with you. It's a thank-you, not a coupon for strangers."

**"Alcohol?"**
> "To be safe with Ontario liquor rules, we set alcohol to not earn and not be discountable. Points come from the food." (See note below.)

## Launch-week promo

- Double points Monday to Thursday all launch week.
- "Join tonight, get a free appetizer on your next visit" (welcome bonus raised to 250 for launch week only, if the owner agrees).
- Table tent with QR on every table.

## Future upsell (Plus)

Signals: heavy delivery-app use; phone-in pickup orders at dinner; "can I text my regulars about the patio opening?"
Plus pitch (later): order-ahead and pickup with "Ready" alerts, SMS to opted-in members, automations ("We miss you"), in-app payments with a small platform fee instead of a marketplace commission.
