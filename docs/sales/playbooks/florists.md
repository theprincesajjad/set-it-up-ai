# Playbook: Florists

Template: `florist` · Wave 3 · Later plan: Plus · Last updated: Oct 21, 2026

**Why they matter:** florists have a loyal, gift-giving customer base and huge spikes (Valentine's Day, Mother's Day). They're often single-owner shops who decide on the spot.

## Who to talk to & when

- **Decision maker:** owner-designer.
- **Best time:** **Tuesday to Thursday, 1:00–3:30 pm**. Mornings are for deliveries and processing fresh stock.
- **Never:** the two weeks before Valentine's Day or Mother's Day, wedding Fridays/Saturdays in summer, Thanksgiving and December holidays.

## Their world & pains

- Wire services (FTD, Teleflora) and online order sites take big cuts.
- Peaks are extreme; the rest of the year can be slow.
- Customers buy for occasions and forget the shop between them.
- Perishable stock: unsold flowers are waste.

## Current tools they use

- POS: Square, Shopify POS, Lightspeed, florist-specific systems.
- Orders: phone, website, wire services, Instagram DMs.
- Loyalty: rarely anything; some paper cards for weekly bouquet buyers.

## Pitch angle

> "Your customers come back for birthdays, anniversaries, Mother's Day. This reminds them you exist between occasions, rewards the weekly bouquet crowd, and puts you on the map for everyone on the street. Free."

## 90-second demo

1. Scan the demo QR; welcome 25 points.
2. Staff: phone number, $65 arrangement → 65 points.
3. Reward: Free single stem (100 pts).
4. Promo: "Friday bouquets: double points."
5. Birthday bonus: show that members get automatic birthday points (a nice tie-in for a florist).

## Objections & answers

**"I already use paper punch cards."** "We'll transfer them."
**"I already use SoPoints / Square Loyalty / Stamp Me."** "No fee, keep your POS, free migration." See switch scripts.
**"I don't want another tablet."** "Your phone works."
**"My customers won't download an app."** "Phone number at the counter. No app."
**"What's the catch?"** "Loyalty is free forever. Later there'll be optional pre-order and pickup tools for your peak days. Never required."
**"Most of my orders are phone or online."** "Award points from the dashboard after the order, or automatically if your Shopify/Square is connected."

## Launch-week promo

- Double points all week.
- Offer a stem to the first 20 sign-ups (owner's choice).
- Donate a bouquet to the **Passport week** prize basket.

## Future upsell (Plus)

Signals: Valentine's and Mother's Day pre-orders chaos; phone orders for pickup; wants to text opted-in customers before key dates.
Plus pitch (later): pre-orders with pickup slots, "Ready" alerts, SMS reminders before Mother's Day to opted-in members.
