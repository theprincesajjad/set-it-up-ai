# Playbook: Fitness Studios

Template: `fitness` · Wave 2 · Later plan: Plus · Last updated: Oct 21, 2026

**Why they matter:** members visit 2–4 times a week, are often local, and buy coffee and smoothies on the way out. Yoga, pilates, spin, climbing and boxing studios bring a young, local crowd onto the network.

## Who to talk to & when

- **Decision maker:** studio owner. Instructors can't sign. Front-desk staff know the owner's class schedule.
- **Best time:** **10:30 am–12:00 pm** or **1:30–4:00 pm** weekdays, between the morning and evening class peaks.
- **Never:** 6–9 am, 5–8 pm, January (busiest month), class change-overs.

## Their world & pains

- Churn: members drift off after 2–3 months.
- Class packs and memberships are handled in a booking tool; loyalty is an afterthought.
- Aggregators (ClassPass) bring traffic but at a low price per visit.
- New-member acquisition is expensive.

## Current tools they use

- Booking/membership: Mindbody, Momence, Marianatek, WellnessLiving, Glofox, ClassPass.
- POS: built into the booking tool, Square.
- Loyalty: rare; maybe "10th class free" or a referral credit.

## Pitch angle

> "Your members come three times a week. This rewards the habit, so they stay longer, and it shows your studio to everyone who joined at the café down the street. Free, and it doesn't replace your booking software."

## 90-second demo

1. Scan the demo QR at "front desk"; welcome 20 points.
2. Staff: phone number → +10 points for attending.
3. Reward: Free drop-in class at 100 (10 visits).
4. Network perk: "Visit 3 shops on the strip, get bonus points." *"Your members already buy smoothies next door."*
5. Dashboard: visits by member; who hasn't been in for 2 weeks.

## Objections & answers

**"My booking tool already does check-ins."**
> "Keep it for bookings. This is the reward layer and the street network on top. Check-in takes 5 seconds by phone number."
**"I already use paper punch cards."** "We'll transfer them."
**"I already use SoPoints / Square Loyalty / Stamp Me."** "No fee, free migration." See switch scripts.
**"I don't want another tablet."** "Front-desk computer or phone."
**"My members won't download an app."** "Phone number at check-in, or a QR scan by the door."
**"What's the catch?"** "Free forever. Later there may be optional Plus tools, like texting opted-in members. Never required."
**"Free classes cost me."**
> "A free drop-in after 10 paid visits fills an empty mat. If you prefer, make the reward a branded water bottle or a free guest pass (which brings a new person in)."
