# Playbook: Grocery, Markets & Convenience

Template: `grocery` · Wave 1 (traffic anchor) · Later plan: Plus · Last updated: Oct 21, 2026

**Why they matter:** the highest visit frequency on the street. Corner stores and independent grocers see neighbours several times a week. They activate fast and fill the network with residents, not just visitors.

## The rule you must know

The template excludes **Tobacco & lottery** (no earn, no discount). Ontario's Smoke-Free Ontario Act restricts promotion of tobacco and vapour products, and lottery sales follow OLG retailer rules. **Also add these as no-earn categories at setup:**

- **Vapes / vapour products** (same rules as tobacco).
- **Alcohol** (Ontario convenience and grocery stores have sold beer, wine and ready-to-drink since 2024; keep alcohol out of earn and rewards, same as bars).
- **Gift cards, prepaid phone cards, transit fares, bill payments** (no margin; points would be a gift).

## Who to talk to & when

- **Decision maker:** the owner, often behind the counter. Family-run; a spouse or sibling may need to agree.
- **Best time:** **10:00 am–12:00 pm** or **2:00–4:00 pm** weekdays.
- **Never:** after-work rush (4:30–7 pm), weekend mornings at grocers.
- **Tip:** be patient with interruptions. Stand aside while they serve customers. Bring the one-page printout in case English is their second language; ask if they'd prefer a rep who speaks their language (track languages on the team).

## Their world & pains

- Tiny margins on most products; competition from chains with big programs (PC Optimum, Scene+ at Sobeys/FreshCo, etc.).
- Customers are neighbours who come in for one or two items.
- No marketing budget; promotion is a sign in the window.
- Long hours, often 7 days a week.

## Current tools they use

- POS: older cash registers, Clover, Square, Lightspeed, specialty grocery systems.
- Loyalty: almost always nothing. Occasionally a paper card for coffee or a "10th sandwich free".

## Pitch angle

> "The chains have PC Optimum and Scene+. Now you have something too, free, and your customers can use it here, at the café and at the bakery. People who come in every day will finally have a reason to buy one more thing."

## 90-second demo

1. Scan the demo QR; welcome 50 points.
2. Staff: phone number, $23 basket that includes a lottery ticket → points only on the groceries. *"Tobacco, lottery and vapes never earn. It's automatic."*
3. Reward: $5 off at 500 points.
4. Promo: "Bonus 50 points on any local produce this week."
5. Network map: nearby shops.

## Objections & answers

**"I already use paper punch cards."**
> "Keep the coffee card idea: we can set an item bonus or stamps for coffee and still give points on groceries."

**"I already use SoPoints / Square Loyalty / Stamp Me."**
> "No fee, free migration, keep your POS." See switch scripts.

**"I don't want another tablet."**
> "No tablet. Your phone, or the POS if it's connected."

**"My customers won't download an app."**
> "Phone number at the till. That's it."

**"What's the catch?"**
> "Loyalty is free forever. Later, optional tools like order-ahead for pickup. Never required."

**"My margins are too thin to give stuff away."**
> "One point per dollar and $5 back after $500 is a 1% thank-you. The chains give similar or more. Set the reward higher if you need to."

**"Customers will abuse it."**
> "Every point has an audit trail, and you can set a minimum spend."
