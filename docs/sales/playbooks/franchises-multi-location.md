# Playbook: Franchises & Multi-Location Owners

Any template · **Founder-led** (reps qualify and pass up) · Later plan: Plus/Pro · Last updated: Oct 21, 2026

**Why they matter:** **unlimited locations are free.** Most competitors price per location (Square Loyalty is priced per location, from about $60/month each when last checked; re-verify). One yes can add 3–10 storefronts across several strips, and seed future neighbourhoods before we launch them.

## Who's in scope

| Type | Example | Who signs | Fit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Local multi-unit independent | A family that owns 3 cafés or 4 bakeries | Owner | **Best fit** |
| Franchisee with local marketing freedom | Owner of 2–5 units of a regional brand | Franchisee, but **check the franchise agreement** | Good, if allowed |
| Regional franchisor | A 10–40 unit Ontario brand | Head office / marketing lead | Founder deal, longer cycle |
| National franchise system with its own app | Big QSR, coffee chains | Head office | Out of scope for phase 1 |

## Who to talk to & when

- **Rep role:** spot it ("Do you have other locations?"), get the owner's name and email, log the lead with a "multi-location" note, and hand it to the founder within 24 hours.
- **Decision maker:** owner or operations lead. For franchisees, the franchisor's marketing team may have to approve.
- **Best time:** book a call; these owners move between locations. Mid-morning weekdays.

## Their world & pains

- Paying per location for loyalty and POS add-ons.
- Want one program across locations, but each store has different staff and habits.
- Franchisees often can't run their own marketing without approval.
- Need reporting by location.

## Current tools they use

- POS: Square, Toast, Lightspeed, Clover, TouchBistro, Shopify POS.
- Loyalty: Square Loyalty, TapMango, Kangaroo Rewards, a branded app, or nothing.

## Pitch angle

> "One program across every location, free, no matter how many. Customers earn at any of your stores, you see results by location, and each store shows up on its neighbourhood's map. We set up every location for you."

## 20-minute demo (founder)

1. One program, multiple locations: points earned at location A, redeemed at location B.
2. Unlimited staff logins per location.
3. Reports by location.
4. POS auto-award per location (confirm the connector is live for their POS).
5. Migration: CSV import of existing members (with consent records).

## Objections & answers

**"My franchise agreement doesn't let me."** "Then let's not. Can you ask your franchisor? We'll give you a one-page summary to send them."
**"We already use Square Loyalty / TapMango / Kangaroo."** "Keep your POS. You'd drop the per-location fees, and we migrate members free." Do the math with them using their actual bill.
**"We already use SoPoints."** See [../scripts/sopoints-switch.md](../scripts/sopoints-switch.md).
**"Paper punch cards?"** "One digital card works at every location. We'll honour old cards."
**"I don't want another tablet at every store."** "No tablets. Existing devices or POS auto-award."
**"Customers won't download an app."** "Phone number or wallet pass, at any location."
**"What's the catch?"** "Loyalty is free forever, for every location. Later, optional Plus/Pro by location. You only pay where you see value."
**"I want my own branded app."** "That's not what we are. Your brand is on your program inside a shared neighbourhood app, and the network sends you customers a branded app can't."

## Recommended program

- Use the vertical template, **one program across all locations**.
- Same rewards everywhere; location-specific promos allowed.
- Named location manager per store, unlimited staff.
- Sign location by location in the rep console so each counts toward its strip's density and activation.

## Launch promo

- Double points at all locations in the week each strip launches.
- "Visit 2 of our locations, get a bonus" (network perk scoped to the brand).

## Future upsell (Plus/Pro)

Signals: order-ahead demand at busy locations, multi-location SMS campaigns, per-location staff permissions (Pro).
Pitch (later): Plus or Pro per location where it's worth it; advanced reports across locations.
