# Playbook: Retail Boutiques

Template: `retail` · Wave 3 (browse retail) · Later plan: Plus · Last updated: Oct 21, 2026

**Why they matter:** boutiques (clothing, home goods, shoes, jewellery, stationery) give customers a reason to stroll the strip. Bigger tickets, lower frequency. They benefit most from traffic the cafés and bakeries create.

## Who to talk to & when

- **Decision maker:** owner. Often works the floor; Saturdays they're always there but too busy.
- **Best time:** **11:00 am–1:00 pm** or **2:00–4:00 pm** Tuesday to Thursday. Many boutiques open at 11 and are quiet until lunch.
- **Never:** Saturdays, December, sale weekends, Boxing week.
- **Tip:** compliment something real. Ask how long they've been on the street.

## Their world & pains

- Competing with online stores and chains.
- Customers browse, leave, and maybe buy online elsewhere.
- They collect emails at checkout but rarely use them well.
- Seasonal stock that needs to move.

## Current tools they use

- POS: Shopify POS (very common), Lightspeed Retail, Square, Clover.
- Loyalty: Shopify loyalty apps (Smile.io-type), Square Loyalty, nothing, paper "spend $X, get $Y" cards.
- Marketing: Instagram, email (Mailchimp, Klaviyo, Shopify Email).

## Pitch angle

> "People come to this street for coffee and bread. This makes sure they walk into your shop too. Free points program, your own customer list, and a spot on the map every shopper on the strip sees."

## 90-second demo

1. Scan the demo QR; welcome 50 points.
2. Staff: phone number + $85 sale → 85 points.
3. Rewards: $5 off (250), 15% off one item (400).
4. Show a gift card purchase earning 0 (gift cards excluded; points come when the card is spent).
5. Network map and feed: "New arrivals" promo visible to members on the strip.

## Objections & answers

**"I already use a Shopify loyalty app / Square Loyalty."**
> "Keep Shopify or Square. We connect to both, so points land automatically, and you can drop the app subscription. The big difference: your program shows up to everyone on this street, not just people already in your shop." Confirm connector status first.

**"I already use paper punch cards."**
> "We'll move them over. You'll finally see who your best customers are."

**"I already use SoPoints / Stamp Me."**
> "No fee here, and free migration." See switch scripts.

**"I don't want another tablet."**
> "None. Your POS or your phone."

**"My customers won't download an app."**
> "No download. Phone number or QR."

**"What's the catch?"**
> "Free forever. Later we'll offer optional tools like reserve-and-pickup. You'll never need them."

**"Discounts cheapen my brand."**
> "Use a 'free gift with purchase' or early access to new arrivals as the reward instead of a % off."
