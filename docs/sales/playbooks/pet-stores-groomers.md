# Playbook: Pet Stores & Groomers

Template: `pet` · Wave 3 · Later plan: Plus (stores) / Pro (groomers) · Last updated: Oct 21, 2026

**Why they matter:** pet owners are fiercely loyal and buy food every 2–6 weeks. Groomers have regular appointments. Dog owners also walk the strip every day.

## Who to talk to & when

- **Decision maker:** owner. Groomers in a pet store may be renting a chair; check who owns the grooming side.
- **Best time:** **10:00 am–12:00 pm** or **1:30–3:30 pm** weekdays.
- **Never:** Saturdays; groomers in the middle of a dog.

## Their world & pains

- Competing with chains (PetSmart, Pet Valu and Global Pet Foods, several with their own rewards programs) and online autoship subscriptions.
- Food is a repeat purchase; the risk is customers switching to online autoship.
- Groomers chase no-shows and juggle bookings by phone and text.
- Many already run "buy 10 bags, get 1 free" frequent-buyer programs, often funded by pet-food brands.

## Current tools they use

- POS: Lightspeed, Square, Clover, Shopify POS.
- Grooming booking: MoeGo, Gingr, Square Appointments, phone and paper.
- Loyalty: brand-sponsored frequent-buyer paper cards (very common), nothing.

## Pitch angle

> "Your customers buy food every few weeks; you just need them to buy it here, not online. This gives them points on every visit, free, and puts you on the map for every dog owner walking this street."

## 90-second demo

1. Scan the demo QR; welcome 50 points.
2. Staff: phone number, $78 bag of food → 78 points.
3. Reward: $5 off at 250.
4. Show the separate Grooming category earning too.
5. Network map: the café that lets dogs in (a nice local touch).

## Objections & answers

**"I already use paper punch cards (brand frequent-buyer cards)."**
> "Keep those if the brand requires their own card; we run alongside them. Our points are your shop's thank-you on everything else."
**"I already use SoPoints / Square Loyalty / Stamp Me."** "No fee, free migration, keep your POS." See switch scripts.
**"I don't want another tablet."** "None needed."
**"My customers won't download an app."** "Phone number at the till."
**"What's the catch?"** "Loyalty is free forever. Later there'll be optional tools for groomers, like drop-off scheduling and reminders."

## Launch-week promo

- Double points all week.
- "Pet birthday club": join and add your pet's birthday.
- Dog treat jar next to the QR stand.

## Future upsell

- **Groomers → Pro:** signals are no-shows, phone tag about pickup, "your dog is ready" calls. Pro (later): drop-off scheduling with reminders, status updates, "ready for pickup" alerts, deposits.
- **Stores → Plus:** signals are phone orders for food pickup. Plus (later): order-ahead and pickup, SMS reminders "time to restock?" to opted-in members.
