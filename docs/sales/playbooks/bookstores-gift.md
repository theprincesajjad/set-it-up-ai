# Playbook: Bookstores & Gift Shops

Template: `bookstore` (use `retail` for pure gift shops) · Wave 3 · Later plan: Plus · Last updated: Oct 21, 2026

**Why they matter:** beloved community anchors. Owners often know the BIA board, host events, and are natural champions. Visit frequency is low, but goodwill and word-of-mouth are high.

## Who to talk to & when

- **Decision maker:** owner. Often a long-time local with strong opinions about "buy local". This is your strongest values-based pitch.
- **Best time:** **10:30 am–12:30 pm** or **2:00–4:00 pm** weekdays.
- **Never:** author events, holiday season (late November to December 24), Saturdays.

## Their world & pains

- Competing with Amazon and Indigo (which has its own program, plum).
- Margins on books are fixed and thin.
- Events (readings, kids' story time) drive traffic but are hard to measure.
- Gift shops depend on seasonal peaks.

## Current tools they use

- POS: Bookmanager (common with Canadian indie bookstores), Shopify POS, Lightspeed, Square.
- Loyalty: paper "buy 10 books, get $X off" cards are very common; some have Bookmanager-based programs.

## Pitch angle

> "You're the kind of shop people want to keep on this street. This is a free points card shared with every independent on the strip, so 'shop local' finally has a card in people's wallets. Your paper card, but digital, and part of something bigger."

## 90-second demo

1. Scan the demo QR; welcome 25 points.
2. Staff: phone number, $42 purchase → 42 points.
3. Reward: $5 off at 200 points.
4. Promo: "Story time Saturday: bonus 25 points for families."
5. Network map; show that the café next door is on it.

## Objections & answers

**"I already use paper punch cards / a Bookmanager program."**
> "Keep the reward, move the card. We'll transfer balances. If you love your Bookmanager setup, we'll run alongside it; phone number entry takes 5 seconds."

**"I already use SoPoints / Square Loyalty / Stamp Me."**
> "No fee, free migration, and you keep your POS." See switch scripts.

**"I don't want another tablet."**
> "None. Use your phone or the till computer."

**"My customers won't download an app."**
> "No app. A phone number at the till."

**"What's the catch?"**
> "Free forever. We make money later from optional extras some shops want. Anything free today stays free."

**"I don't want my customers tracked."**
> "Members choose to join, can mute any shop, and you only see your own customers. We don't sell customer data."

## Launch-week promo

- Double points all week.
- Offer to host the **Passport week** prize draw at the store (great foot traffic).

## Future upsell (Plus)

Signals: special orders by phone and email; "your book is in" calls; event RSVPs.
Plus pitch (later): "Your order is ready" alerts, reserve-and-pickup, SMS to opted-in members about events.
