# Playbook: Auto Shops

Template: `repair_auto` · Wave 4 (future payer) · Later plan: **Pro** · Last updated: Oct 21, 2026

**Why they matter:** highest ticket size on the street ($300–1,500 visits) and the clearest Pro need (quote approvals, deposits, invoices). Low daily traffic, so they're not network anchors, but every auto shop that signs now is a likely payer later.

## Who to talk to & when

- **Decision maker:** owner, often also the service writer at the front. A service manager may champion it.
- **Best time:** **1:30–3:30 pm** weekdays. Mornings are drop-offs, late afternoon is pickups.
- **Never:** 7:30–10 am, 4–6 pm, Saturdays, the spring and fall tire-change rush (roughly April and late October to November).

## Their world & pains

- **Approvals by phone:** "We found a problem with your brakes, it's $640, shall we proceed?" Customers don't answer; cars sit in bays.
- Trust: customers worry about being upsold.
- Seasonal tire swaps create a surge and then silence.
- Competing with dealerships and chains (Canadian Tire, Midas, etc.), some with loyalty programs or Triangle Rewards.

## Current tools they use

- Shop management: Mitchell 1, Tekmetric, Protractor, Shopmonkey, ALLDATA, paper.
- POS: built into shop software, Clover, Square.
- Loyalty: rarely. Sometimes "every 5th oil change free" stickers.

## Pitch angle

> "Your customers come twice a year. This keeps you top of mind between tire swaps, rewards them for coming back to you instead of the dealer, and puts you on the map for everyone on this street. Free."

Then, lightly: "We're also building quote approvals and invoicing for shops like yours. Can I put you down for early input?"

## 90-second demo

1. Scan the demo QR; welcome 100 points.
2. Staff: phone number, $420 brake job → 420 points.
3. Rewards: Free tire rotation (400), $25 off service (1,000).
4. Promo: "Book your winter tire swap this week: 100 bonus points."
5. Customer directory: "See who hasn't been in for 8 months."

## Objections & answers

**"My customers only come twice a year."** "That's why the welcome bonus is big and the first reward is reachable on one brake job."
**"I already use paper punch cards (oil-change stickers)."** "Move them to points; we'll honour existing cards."
**"I already use SoPoints / Square Loyalty / Stamp Me."** "On a $600 repair, a percentage fee is real money. Ours is zero." See switch scripts.
**"I don't want another tablet."** "Service desk computer or your phone."
**"My customers won't download an app."** "Phone number at pickup."
**"What's the catch?"** "Loyalty is free forever. Later, optional Pro: quote approvals, deposits and invoices in the customer's app, at a flat monthly price. You'll decide."
**"I already have shop software."** "Keep it. This is the customer reward and the street. Later, Pro may replace your phone calls, not your shop software."
