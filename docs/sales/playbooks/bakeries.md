# Playbook: Bakeries

Template: `bakery` · Wave 1 (traffic anchor) · Later plan: Plus · Last updated: Oct 21, 2026

**Why they matter:** bakeries have weekly and weekend regulars, lines that create buzz, and a loyal neighbourhood following. Families buying Saturday bread are exactly the customers who then walk to the butcher, florist and bookstore.

## Who to talk to & when

- **Decision maker:** the owner, often the baker. They start very early (4–5 am) and may leave by early afternoon. Ask the counter staff: "When is the owner usually here and not elbow-deep in dough?"
- **Best time:** weekdays **1:00–3:00 pm**, after the lunch rush and before the end-of-day clear-out. Tuesday to Thursday are quieter.
- **Never:** Saturday and Sunday mornings, holiday weeks (Thanksgiving, December, Easter).
- **Tip:** buy something. Compliment a specific item. Many bakeries are family businesses; the decision may involve a partner or parent. Ask "Is there anyone else who'd want to see this?"

## Their world & pains

- Early hours, physical work, almost no time for admin or marketing.
- Daily waste: end-of-day product they'd love to move.
- Seasonal peaks (holidays, wedding season) and slow midweeks.
- Pre-orders for cakes and holiday items handled by phone and paper.
- Often cash-heavy and older POS setups.

## Current tools they use

- POS: Square, Clover, Lightspeed, Shopify POS, older cash registers.
- Loyalty: paper punch cards, nothing at all, occasionally SoPoints or a wallet stamp card.
- Orders: phone, Instagram DMs, email, paper order book.

## Pitch angle

> "Your Saturday regulars are your best customers. This gives them a stamp card they can't lose, lets you tell them when the hot cross buns are in, and puts you on the map with every shop on this street. Free, and I set it up."

## 90-second demo

1. Scan the demo bakery QR. Pass shows "1 of 8 stamps".
2. Staff view: phone number, **+1 stamp**. *"Five seconds, while you bag the bread."*
3. Show a promo: "Fresh croissants after 3 pm: double stamps". *"Move end-of-day stock without slashing prices."*
4. Network map: the café and florist nearby are on it.
5. Dashboard: top regulars, visits by day. *"See your real Saturday crowd."*

## Objections & answers

**"I already use paper punch cards."**
> "We'll honour every paper card. Customers show it, we add the stamps. After launch week, you can stop printing cards."

**"I already use SoPoints / Square Loyalty / Stamp Me."**
> SoPoints: "No percentage fee here, and free migration." Square Loyalty: "Keep Square, drop the per-location fee." Stamp Me/Loopy: "Same no-download card plus the whole street." See the switch scripts.

**"I don't want another tablet."**
> "None needed. Your phone works, or it awards from your POS if it's connected."

**"My customers won't download an app."**
> "Many of your customers are older; that's why the default is phone number at the counter. No app, no email needed."

**"What's the catch?"**
> "Loyalty is free forever. Later there'll be optional paid tools like cake pre-orders with pickup times. Never required."

**"I'm too busy."**
> "You're the busiest person on this street. That's why I do it. Twenty minutes on a Tuesday afternoon."

**"I'm cash only / mostly cash."**
> "Perfect fit. Cash customers can't get points through a card machine anyway. Staff type the phone number."

## Launch-week promo

- Double stamps all week.
- "First 50 to join get a free mini-pastry" if the owner is willing (their cost, their call).
- Paper card transfer.
- Target: 25 members by day 5.

## Future upsell (Plus)

Signals: phone rings off the hook for holiday orders; Instagram DMs for cake orders; paper pre-order book; customers asking "can I reserve a loaf?"
Plus pitch (later): order-ahead with pickup slots and "Your order is ready" alerts. If they take lots of custom-cake orders with deposits, note it: deposits are a Pro feature.
