# Playbook: Bars & Pubs

Template: `bar` · Wave 3 (destination) · Later plan: Plus · Last updated: Oct 21, 2026

**Why they matter:** evening and weekend traffic, local regulars, trivia and game nights. They keep the network alive after 6 pm and drive the "after the shop, grab a pint" path.

## The rule you must know

**Alcohol does not earn points and cannot be discounted or rewarded in our program.** Ontario's AGCO rules restrict inducements tied to alcohol sales. The `bar` template puts Alcohol in a no-earn, no-discount category. **Never** switch it on to close a deal. Points come from food and non-alcoholic drinks. If the owner pushes back, say "We keep it conservative so you never have a licence problem," and log it for the founder. (Legal should confirm the exact current AGCO position; this kit does not give legal advice.)

## Who to talk to & when

- **Decision maker:** owner or GM. The bartender knows when they're in.
- **Best time:** **3:00–5:00 pm** weekdays, before happy hour. Some pubs open at 11 for lunch; 2:30 pm works.
- **Never:** Friday/Saturday nights, during big games, trivia night.

## Their world & pains

- Weeknights are slow; big-game nights are packed.
- Food margins matter because alcohol promotions are restricted.
- Regulars are the business, but they're known by face, not by contact.
- Event promotion lives on Instagram and chalkboards.

## Current tools they use

- POS: TouchBistro, Toast, Lightspeed, Square, Clover.
- Loyalty: mostly none (because of alcohol rules), "mug club" style memberships, wing-night punch cards.
- Marketing: Instagram, chalkboards, Facebook events.

## Pitch angle

> "Your regulars are your business. This gives them a reason to order food on a Tuesday, and it puts you on the map for everyone shopping this street during the day. Free, and it keeps you on the right side of the liquor rules."

## 90-second demo

1. Scan the demo pub QR. Welcome 25 points.
2. Staff: phone number, bill $48 of which $30 is food → 150 points on food only. *"Alcohol is excluded automatically."*
3. Reward: Free snack (150 pts).
4. Promo: "Wing Wednesday: double points on food."
5. Network map: the restaurants and shops nearby.

## Objections & answers

**"Why can't they earn on beer? That's what they buy."**
> "Ontario restricts alcohol inducements. We keep alcohol off the program so you never have to think about it. Food points still bring them in on slow nights."

**"I already use paper punch cards (wing night / mug club)."**
> "Move it to a stamp card on food items. Same idea, no lost cards."

**"I already use SoPoints / Square Loyalty."**
> "No fee here, and keep your POS." See switch scripts. Check their current setup doesn't reward alcohol; don't comment on how they ran it before.

**"I don't want another tablet."**
> "Use the bar phone or your POS tablet."

**"My customers won't download an app."**
> "No download. Phone number at close-out, or the QR on the table."

**"What's the catch?"**
> "Loyalty is free forever. Later there'll be optional tools like pickup ordering. Never required."
