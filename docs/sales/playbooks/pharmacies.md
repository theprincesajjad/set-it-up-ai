# Playbook: Pharmacies (front store only)

Template: `pharmacy` · Wave 2 · Later plan: Plus · Last updated: Oct 21, 2026

**Why they matter:** independent pharmacies are trusted, visited often (especially by seniors and families), and many already hand out paper discount cards. They bring older residents onto the network, a group the other shops struggle to reach.

## The law, in plain words

- **No points, bonuses, discounts or inducements on prescriptions or pharmacy services.** This is Ontario law and Ontario College of Pharmacists (OCP) policy (O. Reg. 264/16 s. 34 and O. Reg. 130/17 s. 6, plus the OCP loyalty points policy; links in docs/research/pharmacies-wellness.md).
- **Front-store items are fine:** vitamins, cosmetics, snacks, household goods, greeting cards.
- The `pharmacy` template creates four categories: **Front store** (earns, discountable), **Prescriptions**, **Pharmacy services** (flu shots, vaccines, med reviews) and **Dispensing fees and compounding** (none of the three earn or discount). These are locked on the server. **You never change them.**
- **Three more locks** the pharmacist will ask about: no points for a *visit* or a stamp (a visit may include a prescription), no referral rewards (a benefit for referring a patient is its own conflict-of-interest rule), and no messages or offers that mention prescriptions, refills, vaccines or a health condition. The cashier ticks "no prescriptions or pharmacy services in this total" on every sale.
- The seniors' discount card in the template says: "Front-store items only. Not valid on prescriptions or pharmacy services."
- We are not their lawyer. Tell the pharmacist: "Please check this against your own OCP obligations; we built it to the policy, but you're the licence holder."

## Who to talk to & when

- **Decision maker:** the owner-pharmacist (designated manager). Front-store managers can champion it but usually can't sign.
- **Best time:** **2:00–4:00 pm** Tuesday to Thursday. Avoid opening, lunch, and the after-work pickup rush (4:30–6:30 pm).
- **Never:** Mondays (prescription backlog), first days of the month, flu-shot clinic days.
- **Banner check:** many independents belong to a banner (e.g. IDA/Guardian, Pharmasave, Remedy'sRx, others). Some banners have their own loyalty program or rules. **Ask: "Does your banner agreement say anything about loyalty programs?"** Log "banner check" in notes. Corporate chain stores (e.g. Shoppers Drug Mart, Rexall corporate stores) are out of scope; the local manager can't sign.

## Their world & pains

- Front store is where margin lives; dispensing fees are squeezed.
- Competing with Shoppers (PC Optimum) on front store with no loyalty of their own.
- Paper seniors' discount cards, "Seniors Day" signs, cards that are hard to track.
- Pharmacist time is precious; anything that takes them away from the dispensary is a no.

## Current tools they use

- Pharmacy software: Kroll, Healthwatch, others (prescription side, we don't touch it).
- Front-store POS: often separate from the dispensary system, or integrated in the pharmacy software.
- Loyalty: paper discount cards, banner program, nothing.

## Pitch angle

> "Your customers carry PC Optimum because nobody gave them a reason to carry yours. This is a free front-store points card and a digital seniors' discount card, with prescriptions blocked by design. It's also on the same map as the bakery and café your customers already visit."

## 90-second demo

1. Show the template categories first: **Prescriptions: no earn, no discount** (locked). *"This is the first thing I want you to see."*
2. Scan the demo QR; welcome 50 points.
3. Staff: phone number, $42 front-store purchase, tick *no prescriptions or pharmacy services in this total* → 42 points. Show a mixed basket where a prescription line earns 0.
4. Show the digital **Seniors 10% off** card with its terms.
5. Network map: other shops nearby.

## Objections & answers

**"Pharmacy regs."**
> "Built in. Prescriptions and pharmacy services are blocked from earning and from discounts. It's front store only, and it's locked so staff can't change it by accident. Please check it against your OCP obligations; we built it to the policy."

**"My banner has a program."**
> "Then check your agreement first. If you're allowed a local program, we work alongside it for front store. If not, no problem."

**"I already use paper discount cards."**
> "We digitize the same card with the same terms. You'll know how many seniors actually use it."

**"I already use SoPoints / Square Loyalty."**
> "No fee here, and we'll move your members. Make sure whatever you use excludes prescriptions." See switch scripts.

**"I don't want another tablet."**
> "Use the front-store computer or a phone. Nothing behind the dispensary counter."

**"My customers won't download an app. Many are seniors."**
> "That's why it's a phone number at the till. Nothing to download, nothing to remember."

**"What's the catch?"**
> "Loyalty is free forever. Later there may be optional tools, like 'your order is ready' texts for front-store pickups. Never for prescriptions."

## Launch-week promo

- Double front-store points all week.
- "Bring your paper seniors' card, get the digital one plus 50 points."
- Sign at the front-store till, **not** at the dispensary counter.
- Do **not** advertise points near the prescription drop-off or pickup area.

## Future upsell (Plus)

Signals: lots of front-store pickup by phone; seasonal promos (cold and flu season front-store items); wants to text opted-in customers about front-store sales.
Plus pitch: SMS and email to opted-in members about front-store offers, a fixed "your order is ready for pickup" text (never names the order), and flu-shot and medication-review booking that stores only that a booking exists. Never tie messages to prescriptions.
