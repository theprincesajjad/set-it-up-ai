# Playbook: Print Shops

Template: `print_shop` · Wave 4 (future payer) · Later plan: **Pro** · Last updated: Oct 21, 2026

**Why they matter:** small-business and student customers who come back for flyers, cards, posters and copies. Every print job starts with a quote and a proof approval, which is a natural Pro fit. They also print things for the other shops on the strip: offer to use them for our counter kits.

## Who to talk to & when

- **Decision maker:** owner, often at the counter or the machines.
- **Best time:** **10:00 am–12:00 pm** or **2:00–4:00 pm** weekdays.
- **Never:** election season, the end of university terms (thesis printing), before big local events.

## Their world & pains

- Quotes and proof approvals by email that get lost.
- Customers come once for a job and vanish.
- Competing with online printers (Vistaprint, etc.) and big-box print centres.

## Current tools they use

- Print MIS: PrintSmith, Printavo (apparel), spreadsheets, email.
- POS: Square, Clover, Shopify POS.
- Loyalty: almost never.

## Pitch angle

> "Your customers are the small businesses on this street. This keeps them coming back to you instead of ordering online, it's free, and every shop on the network sees you. Bonus: we print our counter kits locally, and we'd love to quote with you."

## 90-second demo

1. Scan the demo QR; welcome 25 points.
2. Staff: phone number, $120 flyer job → 120 points.
3. Reward: $10 off at 300.
4. Promo: "Students: double points in April."
5. Network map: every shop on the strip is a potential customer of theirs.

## Objections & answers

**"Most of my jobs are for businesses."** "Points work for any customer with a phone number; the owner of the café earns too."
**"I already use paper punch cards."** "We'll move them."
**"I already use SoPoints / Square Loyalty / Stamp Me."** "No fee, free migration." See switch scripts.
**"I don't want another tablet."** "Counter computer or phone."
**"My customers won't download an app."** "Phone number at pickup."
**"What's the catch?"** "Free forever. Later, optional Pro: quotes and proof approvals in the customer's app, deposits and invoices. Never required."
