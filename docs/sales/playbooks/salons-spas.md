# Playbook: Salons & Spas

Templates: `salon` (hair/nail) and `spa` (spa/wellness) · Wave 2 · Later plan: Pro · Last updated: Oct 21, 2026

**Why they matter:** clients return every 3–8 weeks, spend $50–200 a visit, and talk. Salons have big word-of-mouth networks on the street.

## Who to talk to & when

- **Decision maker:** salon owner. Many stylists rent chairs; they can't sign for the salon. Front-desk staff know the owner's schedule.
- **Best time:** **Tuesday or Wednesday, 10:00 am–12:00 pm** (many salons are closed Mondays). Ask the front desk for a 15-minute gap in the owner's book; you may need to book it like a client.
- **Never:** Friday afternoons, Saturdays, wedding and prom season weekends, before holidays.

## Their world & pains

- No-shows and late cancellations.
- Clients drift to another salon when their stylist leaves.
- Rebooking depends on the stylist remembering to ask.
- Retail products (shampoo etc.) sit on shelves.

## Current tools they use

- Booking/POS: Fresha, Vagaro, Booksy, Square Appointments, GlossGenius, Mindbody (spas), Phorest.
- Loyalty: built into their booking tool (sometimes), paper cards, nothing.
- Marketing: Instagram, booking-tool reminders.

## Pitch angle

> "Your clients come every six weeks. This gives them a reason to keep coming to you, not the place down the street, and to buy their products here too. It's free, it runs next to your booking app, and it puts you in front of everyone on this strip."

## 90-second demo

1. Scan the demo QR; welcome 10 points.
2. Staff: phone number → +10 points (per-visit program). *"Every visit counts the same, whether it's a trim or colour."*
3. Reward: $10 off a service at 60 points (6 visits).
4. Products category earns too: "buy shampoo, get points."
5. Network map: the café next door where clients wait for colour to process.

## Objections & answers

**"My booking app already has loyalty."**
> "If you use it and love it, great; keep it. Most salons we meet don't use it. Ours is free, shared with the street, and staff can award in 5 seconds at checkout."
**"I already use paper punch cards."** "We'll transfer them."
**"I already use SoPoints / Square Loyalty / Stamp Me."** "No fee; if you're on Square Appointments, we connect to Square." See switch scripts.
**"I don't want another tablet."** "Front-desk computer or your phone."
**"My clients won't download an app."** "Phone number at checkout."
**"What's the catch?"** "Loyalty is free forever. Later there may be optional Pro tools, like deposits for long appointments. Never required."
**"Discounts make my services look cheap."**
> "Swap the $10 off for a free add-on (deep conditioning, nail art) as a custom reward."

## Recommended program

| Setting | `salon` default | `spa` default | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Earn type | 10 pts per visit | 1 pt per $ | Salons with very different service prices may prefer per $ |
| Welcome bonus | 10 | 50 | |
| Reward | $10 off a service at 60 | $20 off at 1,000 | |
| Categories | Services, Products (both earn) | Services (earn) | Add "Gift cards" as no-earn |
| Optional | Birthday reward; referral bonus (both earn) | | Referrals work very well in salons |

## Launch-week promo

- Double points all week.
- Referral push: "Bring a friend, you both get 20 points."
- QR stand at reception and a small card at each station.

## Future upsell (Pro)

Signals: no-show complaints; asking for deposits; phone tag about appointment times.
Pro pitch (later): deposits, drop-off/appointment reminders, staff permissions, invoices. Note: if they already love their booking tool, Pro may not fit; log it honestly.
