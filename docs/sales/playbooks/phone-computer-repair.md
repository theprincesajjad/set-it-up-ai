# Playbook: Phone & Computer Repair

Template: `repair_electronics` · Wave 4 (future payer, sign from day 1) · Later plan: **Pro** · Last updated: Oct 21, 2026

**Why they matter:** they're not daily-traffic shops, but they're the **most likely to pay for Pro later**. Every repair shop chases quote approvals by phone. Sign them free now, build trust, and flag them as Pro candidates.

## Who to talk to & when

- **Decision maker:** owner, usually at the bench or the counter.
- **Best time:** **11:00 am–1:00 pm** or **2:00–4:00 pm** weekdays. Most open at 10 or 11.
- **Never:** first hour after opening (drop-offs), Saturdays, back-to-school week, the week after a new iPhone launch.

## Their world & pains

- **Chasing approvals:** "Your screen and battery need replacing, it's $180, shall we go ahead?" Phone tag eats hours.
- Customers don't pick up repaired devices; storage fills up.
- One-time customers: people come once when something breaks, and forget the shop.
- Price competition and fear of "who can I trust?"

## Current tools they use

- Repair software: RepairDesk, RepairShopr, CellSmart, spreadsheets, paper tickets.
- POS: built into repair software, Square, Clover.
- Loyalty: almost never. Sometimes "10% off your next repair" cards.

## Pitch angle

> "People only come when something breaks, so they forget you. This keeps you in their wallet, free, alongside every other shop on this street. And it's built by people who know the approvals headache. That's where we're going next."

Do **not** sell Pro. Mention it lightly: "We're building approvals and tickets for shops like yours; I'd love your input." Log interest.

## 90-second demo

1. Scan the demo QR; welcome 50 points.
2. Staff: phone number, $149 screen repair → 149 points.
3. Rewards: Free screen protector (150), $20 off next repair (500).
4. Promo: "Bring a friend: both get 50 points" (referrals; trust matters in repair).
5. *Optional, if Pro is in preview:* show the quote-approval mock-up and ask "Would this save you time?" Never promise a date.

## Objections & answers

**"Customers only come once."**
> "That's the point: the welcome bonus and the screen protector reward give them a reason to come back for the case, the battery, the next phone."
**"I already use paper punch cards."** "We'll transfer them."
**"I already use SoPoints / Square Loyalty / Stamp Me."** "No fee, free migration." See switch scripts.
**"I don't want another tablet."** "Your counter computer or phone."
**"My customers won't download an app."** "Phone number when they pick up."
**"What's the catch?"**
> "Loyalty is free forever. Later we'll offer Pro: tickets, quote approvals in the app, deposits and invoices, for a flat monthly price. If it saves you phone time, you'll want it. If not, you'll never pay."
**"My repair software already does this."**
> "Great for tickets. This is the customer-facing side, the reward and the street network. Keep both."
