# Playbook: Barbers

Template: `barber` · Wave 2 · Later plan: Pro · Last updated: Oct 21, 2026

**Why they matter:** clients come every 2–4 weeks like clockwork. Barbers know everyone on the street, decide on the spot, and love a simple "8 cuts, 9th free."

## Who to talk to & when

- **Decision maker:** shop owner (often also cutting). Chair renters can't sign for the shop.
- **Best time:** **Tuesday to Thursday, 10:00 am–12:00 pm** or **2:00–4:00 pm**. Wait until a chair is free; never interrupt a cut.
- **Never:** Friday afternoon, Saturdays, before long weekends, back-to-school week.
- **Tip:** if you need a haircut, get one there. Not required, but it works.

## Their world & pains

- Walk-ins vs appointments; no-shows on busy days.
- Paper punch cards get lost or "found" with extra stamps.
- Competition from new shops opening nearby.

## Current tools they use

- Booking/POS: Booksy, Square Appointments, Squire, Fresha, cash.
- Loyalty: paper punch cards (very common), nothing.

## Pitch angle

> "You already do '8 cuts, 9th free' on paper. This is the same card on their phone. Nobody loses it, nobody fakes it, and every client sees the café and the shops around you. Free."

## 90-second demo

1. Scan the demo QR. Card shows 0/8 stamps.
2. Staff: phone number → +1 stamp.
3. Card at 8/8 → **Free cut** pops up.
4. Referral: "Bring a buddy, you both get a stamp."
5. Network map.

## Objections & answers

**"I already use paper punch cards."** "Keep the deal, lose the paper. We transfer old cards."
**"I already use SoPoints / Square Loyalty / Stamp Me."** "No fee, free migration, keep Square." See switch scripts.
**"I don't want another tablet."** "Your phone. Five seconds between cuts."
**"My customers won't download an app."** "Phone number or a QR scan at the chair."
**"What's the catch?"** "Free forever. Later there'll be optional Pro tools like deposits for no-shows. Never required."
**"Most of my clients pay cash."** "That's fine. Points don't depend on how they pay."

- "Transfer your paper card + get a bonus stamp."
- Mirror sticker with the QR code at each chair.
