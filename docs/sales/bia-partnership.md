# BIA Partnership Playbook

Last updated: Oct 21, 2026 · Owner: founder (reps support)

Toronto has 85+ Business Improvement Areas covering 30,000+ businesses (City of Toronto, see PRD). A BIA is a ready-made cluster with a manager, a board, a newsletter, a marketing budget and an events calendar. **Winning the BIA first makes every merchant conversation warmer.**

## What we want from the BIA (phase 1)

1. **An intro** in the BIA newsletter and/or email to members (written by us, sent by them).
2. **A seat at a members' meeting** or board meeting for 10 minutes.
3. **Permission to say** "supported by [BIA name]" on our one-pager (only once they agree, in writing).
4. **A member list** (business names and public contact info only; we don't need anything private).
5. **A co-branded Passport week** in launch week 4.

## What the BIA gets

| For the BIA | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| A free digital loyalty network for every member | No cost to members, no fees, done-for-you setup |
| A "shop local" programme they can point to | Members on one app, visible on a neighbourhood map |
| **Measurable visit data** | Aggregated, anonymous reports: members, visits, cross-shop visits, campaign results |
| Turnkey campaigns | "Points Weekend" and "Passport Week" packages, run through the app |
| Less work | We do the signups, training, posters and reporting |

## The pitch (founder, 10 minutes)

> "Every BIA spends on events and marketing, and it's hard to prove what it did. We're putting your members on one free loyalty app, street by street. Customers who join at the café see the bakery, the barber and the bookstore. Members pay nothing, we set it up, and you get a report showing how many people visited, how many visited more than one shop, and what your events actually did."

**Then show:** the network map with demo shops on their street, a sample visit report, the Passport week mock-up.

**Then ask:** "Would you be willing to introduce us in your next newsletter, and let us run a Passport week with you in [month]?"

## Who to pitch & when

- **BIA executive director / manager** first (often part-time on smaller BIAs). Email, then call, then meet in person on the strip.
- **Board:** usually local owners. If the manager likes it, ask for 10 minutes at the next board meeting. A board member who's already signed up is your best advocate.
- **Timing:** BIAs plan events and budgets well ahead. Approach in the fall for spring campaigns, and in late winter for summer and fall campaigns. Avoid the weeks before their big festival.

## Campaign packages (BIA-paid)

Prices proposed in the PRD; test them. In the product, the street-wide campaign feature (`campaigns.neighbourhood`) is planned to open once the network reaches roughly 100 merchants. **For our first strip, offer the first Passport Week free as a pilot** in exchange for the intro, newsletter and a case-study quote. Paid packages start with the second campaign or the second strip.

## Prize draw rules (Passport Week)

Canadian contest law has specific requirements. Every draw needs: **a no-purchase-necessary way to enter**, a **mathematical skill-testing question** for the winner, published rules (sponsor, dates, how to enter, prize value, odds), and a fair draw. Have the founder get the rules template reviewed once by a lawyer. Prizes donated by merchants shouldn't include alcohol, tobacco or cannabis.

## MOU outline (1–2 pages, non-binding except confidentiality and data)

1. **Parties:** [BIA name] and Local Loyalty [legal entity].
2. **Purpose:** offer BIA members a free loyalty network and run co-branded campaigns.
3. **Local Loyalty will:** provide the free merchant program; sign up and train members; produce posters, decals and social kits; deliver visit reports; run agreed campaigns.
4. **BIA will:** introduce Local Loyalty to members (newsletter, meeting); share public member contact info; co-promote agreed campaigns; review campaign materials within 5 business days.
5. **Branding:** each party approves use of its name and logo in advance.
6. **Data and privacy:** BIA receives aggregated, anonymous data only. Local Loyalty complies with PIPEDA and CASL. No customer personal information is shared with the BIA.
7. **No exclusivity on members:** members choose freely whether to join; the BIA doesn't endorse any member's choice to switch from another provider.
8. **Fees:** pilot campaign free; later campaigns per the package price list, invoiced 50% on signing, 50% after the report.
9. **Term:** 12 months, either party may end with 30 days' notice.
10. **Contacts** and signatures.
