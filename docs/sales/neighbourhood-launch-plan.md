# Neighbourhood Launch Plan

Last updated: Oct 21, 2026

**One strip at a time.** A customer who joins at the café should see the bakery, the barber and the repair shop on day one. That needs 30–40 shops within a 10-minute walk (about 800 m), live at the same time.

---

## 1. Strip selection scorecard

Score each candidate 1–5 on each line after **walking it** (not from Google Maps). Weighted max = 5.0. Launch the highest score, but a strip scoring under 3 on **BIA engagement** waits, whatever its total.

| Criterion | Weight | 5 = | 1 = |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Independent density within a 10-min walk | 20% | 80+ storefronts, 40+ fit our ICP | Under 40 storefronts, mostly chains |
| Daily-visit mix | 15% | 8+ cafés, bakeries, grocers | Fewer than 3 |
| Service / high-ticket mix | 10% | 5+ repair, auto, alterations, print | None |
| BIA engagement | 20% | Manager returns calls in 48 h, active newsletter, regular events | No reply after 3 tries |
| Competitor coverage | 15% | No SoPoints or other shared program visible | Many shops already on SoPoints |
| Foot traffic & residential density | 10% | Busy sidewalks weekdays and weekends, homes around it | Car-dependent, quiet weekdays |
| Rep logistics | 5% | On a subway/streetcar line, reps live nearby | Hard to reach |
| Community cohesion | 5% | Owners know each other, local media and Instagram accounts | Transient, high vacancy |

**Count competitor coverage on the walk:** SoPoints stickers or cards, Square Loyalty prompts, wallet stamp QR codes, paper cards. Write it down per shop.

### Candidate strips (hypotheses to test on the walk; not data)

| Strip | Why it might work | Watch out for |

| **Kensington Market** | Most independent strip in the city, huge foot traffic, high network value | Cash-heavy, sceptical of tech and anything that feels corporate; slower yes |
| **Little Italy** (College St) | Restaurants, bars, cafés, nightlife | Lower daytime frequency; alcohol no-earn |

**Recommendation:** walk Roncesvalles, Bloor West Village and Leslieville first (the PRD's top candidates), score all three, and call the three BIA managers in the same week. Pick the strip where the **BIA says yes** and SoPoints is thinnest.

## 2. How to walk and map a strip

**Time:** one 4-hour weekday shift for two reps (one per side of the street), plus one Saturday hour to see weekend traffic.

1. **Draw the box.** Pick the anchor (busiest café or bakery). Draw a 10-minute walk (~800 m) along the main street and one block into side streets.
2. **Walk both sides.** For every storefront, record:
   - Business name, vertical (use the product's vertical list), address
   - Independent / franchise / corporate chain
   - Hours posted on the door
   - POS visible at the counter (Square, Clover, Toast, Lightspeed, Shopify, TouchBistro, other)
   - Loyalty visible (paper cards, SoPoints, Square prompt, wallet QR, none)
   - Busy or quiet at time of walk
   - Vacant units (count them; high vacancy lowers the score)
3. **Enter every storefront as a lead** in the rep console, stage `new`, with the neighbourhood set. Don't pitch on the mapping walk unless the owner starts the conversation.
4. **Tag anchors:** 3–5 shops with lines, great reviews, or owners who are known on the street (ask the BIA manager).
5. **Tag waves:** use [icp-and-segmentation.md](icp-and-segmentation.md) to assign wave 1–4.
6. **Count targets:** you need **40+ ICP-fit leads** to safely land 30–40 signed.

## 3. The 4-week launch calendar

Assumes 2–3 reps doing 3 shifts a week each, and the founder on BIA and anchors.

### Week 0 — BIA intro & anchors
- BIA newsletter intro goes out (Monday).
- Founder and lead rep visit **anchors** in person; aim to sign 3–5 by Friday.
- Reps finish mapping; every lead in the console.
- Cold-email touch 1 to every lead with a published email (CASL rules apply).
- Print counter kits for the expected signups (QR stands, launch-week signs, stuffers).

### Week 1 — Signups (waves 1 and 2)
- Calls in the morning (quiet hours per playbook), walk-ins in the afternoon.
- Every yes gets the 20-minute setup visit on the spot or within 48 hours.
- Start conversations with pharmacies and multi-location owners early (slower yes).
- Email touch 2 (day 3).

### Week 2 — Signups (waves 3 and 4) & day 7 calls
- Destination and service shops; pitch with the live count ("18 shops on already").
- Day 7 calls for week-0 and week-1 signups.
- Email touch 3 (day 7) with social proof.
- Confirm launch-week date with every shop; drop off launch signs.

### Week 3 — Launch week: double points on the whole street
- Every shop runs **double points / double stamps** all week.
- Staff ask every customer (counter script in [customer-acquisition.md](customer-acquisition.md)).
- Reps walk the strip daily: check QR stands are visible, staff are asking, answer questions.
- Neighbourhood Instagram, BIA channels, local press pitch (see customer acquisition).
- Email touch 4 (breakup) to anyone still undecided; last walk-ins.

### Week 4 — Passport Week (with the BIA)
- Customers visit 5 shops to enter the prize draw (prize basket donated by merchants; contest rules apply, see [bia-partnership.md](bia-partnership.md)).
- In-app network perk: bonus points when a customer visits their 3rd shop on the strip.
- Day 30 calls begin for week-0 signups.

### Week 5–8 — Activation push and the gate
- Day 7 and day 30 calls for everyone; visits for anyone under 10 members.
- BIA visit report delivered (week 5).
- Gate review at the end of week 8 (day 30 after launch week for most shops).

## 5. Gates before the next strip

**Don't open a second strip until all of these are true** (reviewed by the founder at the end of week 8):

| Gate | Threshold | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Activation** | **≥ 60% of signed shops activated** (25+ members with a visit) | PRD success metric; proves onboarding works |
| Cross-shop members | ≥ 15% of members have points at 2+ shops (heading to the PRD's 30% by month 6) | Proves the network effect, not just 35 separate programs |
| Merchant retention | ≤ 10% of signed shops have stopped awarding | Proves staff keep using it |
| CAC | ≤ ~$90 per merchant | Unit economics (see [rep-program.md](rep-program.md)) |
| BIA | Visit report delivered; BIA willing to give a quote or reference | Fuel for the next BIA pitch |

**If we miss a gate:** stay on the strip. Find out why (QR stands hidden? staff not asking? POS awarding broken? wrong mix of shops?), fix the onboarding playbook, and re-run activation for 2–4 weeks. Only then move on.

**Next strip choice:** adjacent strips first (e.g. Roncesvalles → Bloor West Village or Parkdale), so members who already have the app find new shops nearby.
