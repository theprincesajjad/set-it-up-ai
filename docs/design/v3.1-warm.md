## 1. The direction

The founder looked at the v3 build and said "Everything looks so bland". The cause was a monochrome rule (grey on white, black primary buttons, one orange accent, no colour, no imagery) plus demo data that showed zeros. Of three directions, they picked **A, Main Street Warm, with a colour per trade**.

What it is:

- **Warm canvas.** Cream `#FBF6EE` canvas, sand `#F3E9DA` sidebar, white cards, hairline `#EADCC8`, ink `#2A1E14`.
- **Terracotta primary** `#C8452C` with white text and a soft glow under the main call to action. It replaces every black button. Deep forest `#1F5C4A` is the secondary. Points orange is for points only.
- **Fraunces and Inter.** Fraunces Bold for page titles, card titles and big numbers; Inter for everything else; Bricolage Grotesque only for the wordmark. Fraunces does not cover Devanagari, Gurmukhi or Nastaliq, so those headings fall back to the Noto script at the same weight.
- **Colourful tiles.** Pastel tile pairs (peach, mint, butter, lilac, sky, rose) with matching deep text. Stat tiles carry a sparkline. Needs-you rows and quick actions sit in coloured icon tiles. Charts highlight one bar and mute the rest.
- **Lucide icons in coloured tiles, no emoji.** The sidebar has coloured glyphs; the active item is a white pill.
- **One awning stripe per page**, terracotta and white, plus the category illustrations from `apps/web/public/categories`.
- **Human copy.** A greeting, one sentence comparing today with a usual day, "3 waiting" style hints.
- **Warm dark mode.** Espresso `#1A1410` canvas, `#241C16` cards, the same pastel hues at about 18% over the card with light text. It is as colourful as light mode, not grey.
- **Per-trade colour.** Every vertical wears one accent family. The layout is identical in every trade; only the accent, the category illustration, the copy and the numbers change.

What the trade colour touches: the sidebar active glyph, the greeting name, the highlighted bar, the first stat tile, the tip strip under the chart, stamps, the avatar tile on the counter, and the pass gradient. Primary buttons stay terracotta in every trade.

### 2.4 Shape and elevation

Cards 22, stat tiles 20, quick tiles 18, icon tiles 12 to 14, pills full, keypad keys 18, pass card 26 to 28.
Card shadow `0 1px 0 #EADCC8, 0 8px 24px rgb(0 0 0 / .04)`. Primary glow `0 8px 20px rgb(200 69 44 / .35)`. Dark mode: no shadow, a 1px `#3B2F25` line.
Awning stripe: terracotta and white, 28px stripes, 10 to 14px high, bottom corners rounded 8.

### 2.5 Dark-mode formulas

- Tile fill = hue mixed 18% over `#241C16`; tile text = the `Dark text` column.
- Trade accent in dark = the family accent lifted to HSL lightness 62 (saturation at least 55); soft = that accent at 20% over the card; deep = the accent at lightness 80.
- Highlight bars use the lifted accent; muted bars `#4A3E33`.

**Patterns adopted**

1. Greeting, date, one comparison sentence at the top of Today. hilos, Square.
2. Four stat tiles, label then one number then a delta. hilos, Square, Mercury.
3. Quick actions as a short row of icon plus one line. Snowflake.
4. Pass progress as a sentence plus filled and dashed stamps. Chick-fil-A, Sweatcoin, ShopBack.
5. Keypad: number display on top, one primary button, validation before enabling. Swiggy, Quo.
6. Hero: outcome headline, one primary verb used everywhere, friction line under it, a preview that is the product. Beside, Shopify.

Where winners diverge: density (Mercury dense, hilos airy). We follow hilos, because the audience is a shop owner at a counter.

Notes for the build:

- The layout of Today is one template (sidebar 248, main 32 padding, awning, header, four stat tiles, Needs you 684 plus chart 428, Up next 684 plus Quick actions 428). - Illustrations: coffee, beauty, eat and repair are the files in `apps/web/public/categories`. The studio kettlebell, pharmacy bottle and pet paw are stand-ins drawn in the same flat style because the category set has no art for them. They need real assets (`services` is a hard hat and does not fit).
- Pharmacy copy follows `packages/shared/src/pharmacy.ts`: points are front store only, the Needs-you row "Prescriptions earn no points" is a till reminder, and no row mentions prescriptions, refills or medication (rule `no_dispensary_text`). An earlier draft had refill reminders and was rewritten.
- RTL: the whole layout mirrors (sidebar on the right, pills on the left, icon at the start of each row); digits stay Latin.
- The marketing hero and the demo copy use invented shop names (Pinecone Coffee, Bloom Hair Studio, Nawab Biryani, Stride Yoga and Strength, Lakeshore Pharmacy, Waggle Pet Spa, Northline Auto, Fade and Blade Barbers). No photography.
- Anything that looks like emoji in the mock (sun, hand wave, cup in the stamps) is a Lucide icon or an illustration here.
