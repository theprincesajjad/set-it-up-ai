# Clear theme, rev 6: design spec audit and final layout spec

Owner: Design. Date: 2026-10-28. Status: **audit of rev 5, fixes applied in the rev 6 mock, ready for the build team.** Nothing in this change touches app code.

Scope. The founder approved the rev 5 direction and asked for the spacing, placement, symmetry and grids to be checked and corrected. This document is that audit. Every value below was measured with Playwright (`getBoundingClientRect` and computed styles) on the HTML mock at 1440, 1180 (counter) and 390, in English, Urdu (RTL) and dark; nothing was eyeballed. Section 1 lists what was wrong and what changed, ranked by visual impact, each with the principle it serves and the re-measured value after the fix. Section 2 is the layout spec the build team implements against. Colours, glass, font, copy and structure are unchanged from rev 5, except where a line in section 1 says so; the one copy change is the second "Needs you" row, which now reads "Haven't been in as often as usual".

Files (scratch, not committed): `/tmp/claude-0/-home-user-set-it-up-ai/5a1e2619-40bd-5f60-81fb-50d449557f59/scratchpad/clear/v6/`: `mock.src.html` (source of truth, built into `mock.html` by `node build.js`), `measure.js` (per-screen measurement dump, `node measure.js "s=today" 1440 900`), `overlay.js` (grid overlays), `shoot.js`, the rev 6 PNGs listed in section 3 and the raw before (`m-*.json`) and after (`after/*.json`) measurements.

## 1. Audit: measured, problem, change, why, verified

Principles used in the "why" column: **rhythm** (one 4/8 spacing scale, repeated), **alignment** (edges on the column grid, baselines on the 4px grid), **optical balance** (equal insets, centred controls), **concentricity** (inner radius = outer radius − inset), **hierarchy** (size steps mean something), **consistency** (same kind, same dimensions), **accessibility** (44px targets, legible line height). Ranked by visual impact: 1 to 12 are visible at a glance, 13 to 30 are visible on inspection, 31 onward are hygiene the build team would otherwise have to guess at.

Accepted as is (measured, judged fine): chart bars are fractional widths (27.3 at 1440) because 12 bars with 6px gaps divide a 393px plot; bars are filled shapes, not hairlines, so sub-pixel widths do not blur. The counter page scrolls by 40px at 1180 × 820 because the right column is 652 in a 612 content area; the keypad and every primary control are visible without scrolling. Stat-card icon tiles are not concentric with the card corner (20 − 16 would be 4); the concentric rule is applied to shapes that fill their parent (buttons in frames, tabs, segments, sheet cards), not to small tiles near a corner.

## 2. Final layout spec (rev 6)

### 2.1 Grid

| Breakpoint | Page margin | Content width | Columns | Column | Gutter | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1440 (desktop) | 12 to the viewport for the chrome; content starts at x 268 (sidebar 12 + 244 + 12) and ends at 1428 | 1160 | 8 | 131 | 16 | Stat cards span 2 columns (278); Needs you 5 (719), chart column 3 (425); full-width cards 8. Cards that bleed under the floating sidebar start at x 120 and pad their content by `--bl` (148) so the visible content column is still 268. |
| 1180 (counter) | 0 (full-bleed bars) | 1180 | 2 panels: 440 + 740 | – | – | Panel inset 24 top/bottom, 32 sides; top bar 64; dock 96. |
| 390 (phone) | 16 | 358 | 2 | 171 | 16 | Chrome (top capsules, tab bar, map sheet) uses the same 16 margin. Full-width cards span both columns. |

Vertical rhythm: a 4px baseline grid; every line height, control height, padding and gap is a multiple of 4 (8 preferred). Page top: toolbar bottom + 24 (desktop 88, counter 88, phone 72). Header block 56 (title 32, 4, date 20), then 24 to the first card. Cards are 16 apart.

### 2.2 Spacing tokens

`space-1` 4 · `space-2` 8 · `space-3` 12 · `space-4` 16 · `space-5` 20 · `space-6` 24 · `space-8` 32.

Use: 4 title → subtitle, chip icon gap, tab label gap; 8 between sibling controls, inside stat cards, control → control in a toolbar; 12 icon → text in rows and tiles → label, between sheet cards, between large (≥ 44) controls; 16 card padding, card gutter, page gutter (phone), row inline inset; 20 pass-card padding, dock padding; 24 page header gaps, counter panel top/bottom; 32 counter panel sides. Chrome insets: desktop chrome 12 from the viewport, sidebar inner padding 10, toolbar inner padding 10, glass frames (CTA, tab bar) 4, segmented track 2.

### 2.3 Radius scale and concentric rule

`r-1` 4 (checkbox) · `r-2` 8 (badge) · `r-3` 10 (32px tile, brand mark, 32px pill-less control) · `r-4` 12 (36px tile, 40px square button, counter key) · `r-5` 16 (sheet cards, 56px counter dock button) · `r-6` 20 (page cards) · `r-7` 26 (toolbar 52, CTA frame) · `r-8` 30 (sidebar, pass card) · `r-9` 32 (sheet, tab bar, map sheet). Pills: radius = height / 2.

Concentric rule: when a rounded shape fills its rounded parent, inner radius = outer radius − inset. In force: toolbar 26 − 10 = 16 (search field, 32 tall); CTA frame 26 − 4 = 22 (button); tab bar 32 − 4 = 28 (tab); segmented track: pill in pill; sheet 32 − 16 = 16 (cards); map sheet 32 − 16 = 16 (list group; the search field is a 40px pill); top capsule 22 − 2 = 20 (map/list segments). Not applied to small tiles that sit near a corner (stat tiles, row tiles).
