# Customer and marketing QA matrix

One row per (screen x state) for every customer-facing and marketing screen: the customer web pass (`apps/web/src/app/(pass)`), the marketing and legal pages (`(marketing)`) and the Expo customer app (`apps/mobile/src/app`, counter excluded). Built by the vqa-cust team on a fresh seed (API 4790) and a production build (web 3790, `NEXT_DIST_DIR=.next-final-cust`), every state driven through the API or the page itself, screenshotted, compared with the Figma PNG (`scratchpad/figma/<slug>/`) and the canvas (`docs/design/canvas/*.dc.html`), and judged against the purpose line from `docs/research/<slug>.md`.

Side-by-sides (Figma left, built right) are in `scratchpad/shots/final-cust/customer/sbs/`, raw shots in `.../raw/`, named `<id>__<width>__<lang>__<light|dark>.png`. The Width, Languages and Light / dark columns list what was actually shot for that row (derived from the files).

## How a row was judged

- Every shot also runs an automatic audit in the page: horizontal overflow, off-canvas elements, text contrast below WCAG AA (4.5, or 3 for large text), single-line ellipsis truncation, doubled full stops and raw message keys. Overflow and off-canvas came back 0 on every web shot.
- The **Final build reviewed** column records the last pass: every row re-shot on the build above in the widths, languages (en and ur on every row) and light / dark listed, every image looked at (contact sheets for all rows, full-size side-by-sides against the Figma PNG for every frame row), and each "intentional difference" reason checked again. `full` means all of those shots exist (the path pattern lists them). Rows whose result starts with "fixed in the final pass" had a regression or new defect found in this pass, fixed and re-shot.
- A person looked at the side-by-side for rows with a frame, and at contact sheets of the rest in English, Urdu (right to left, Nastaliq line height) and dark. Hindi and Punjabi were spot checked on 5 web pages and 5 app screens (below).
- Result values: **match** (no difference that matters), **fixed: what** (a mismatch or defect was found and fixed in this pass, then re-shot), **intentional difference: why** (kept on purpose, reason given).

## Notes on how the shots were made

- **Payment forms.** The QA build has no Stripe publishable key, so every payment form is the dev "Test payment" box that calls the fake provider; the Stripe Payment Element cannot be drawn offline. Every payment state (order, split, intent, setup, quote deposit, invoice, tip, catering deposit) was driven to its paid state through that box.
- **Fixed bars in full-page shots.** A full-page screenshot draws `position: fixed` bars (the sticky Join button, the cart bar, the filter bar) once, partway down the page; that is the screenshot, not the layout.
- **Hindi, Urdu and Punjabi dates.** Dates are now formatted from the shared name tables (commit 6a5a196), so the headless Chromium without Punjabi ICU data and the server render agree and there is no hydration warning; the old "M10 25" note no longer applies.
- **One-time and short-lived states.** Review links answer once (their unanswered and answered states are both in the review rows), held bookings last 10 minutes (each shot books a fresh slot and cancels it), redeem codes last 10 minutes (each shot makes a code and cancels it), and the fake payments provider forgets its intents when the API restarts (the payment states were created after the last restart).
- **Fake payments provider.** Pay pages and held bookings were created after the last API restart; when the OTP limit forced a restart, the payment states were re-created and the pay rows re-shot.
- **Hindi and Punjabi spot checks** (light, 390): web `shop-nawab-biryani-house`, `menu-nawab-biryani-house`, `j-pinecone`, `q-open`, `for-shoppers`; app `m-wallet`, `m-card`, `m-discover`, `m-settings-privacy`, `m-tiffin-plans`. They read correctly (the Gurmukhi hero line height is tight but not clipped). One observation: the restaurant menu category names (Biryani, Curries, Tandoor, Breads) show in English in hi and pa; they are translated in ur only.
