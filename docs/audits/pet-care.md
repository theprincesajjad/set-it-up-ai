# Pet care audit (Pawsh Grooming, Wag Wing Dog Daycare, Tail & Kibble)

Reviewed Oct 27, 2026 on the shared stack. Owner = a groomer/daycare/food-store owner; customer = dog owner with two dogs, one with an expired bordetella, grooming every 6 weeks, daycare twice a week, a 25 lb bag monthly. Screenshots: `/tmp/claude-0/-home-user-set-it-up-ai/5a1e2619-40bd-5f60-81fb-50d449557f59/scratchpad/audit/pet-care/`. Verified = I saw it; opinion is marked.

## 1. Verdict
- **Owner:** would not pay today as a self-serve signup, because a new pet shop gets none of the pet tools (they are admin-switched). Inside the seeded demo shops the daycare count and food counter are genuinely good and I would pay $39 for them.
- **Customer:** would use the vaccine upload and food card if the shop handed them the link; nothing leads a new customer there, and the shopper app has no pet features at all.
- **Biggest blocker:** pets are not connected to the calendar. Booking and checking out a groom never touches the pet, so cadence, rebooking and the groom list run on a parallel track that only moves via "Send report card" or "Log a groom".

## 2. What works (keep)
- Daycare today (Wag Wing): in the room 2 of 16, ratio vs limit, packs low, and an expired-bordetella dog shown as Blocked with the reason in red. Clear and fast. `wag-daycare.png`
- Food counter (Tail & Kibble): pick customer, pick bag, tap Add bag; numbered circles; "Give the free bag" greyed until earned; Undo last bag. Per-bag-size card matches how chains do buy 12 get 1. `kibble-after-punch.png`
- Customer vaccine entry on phone (`/me/pets`): add two dogs in about 8 s, per-vaccine date and photo, status tags (Current, N days left, Expired), clean at 390, accessible button names. `c-pets-vaccines.png`
- Shop sees the customer's pets the moment the owner joins the shop, with "View proof" on an uploaded certificate. `pawsh-pets-maple.png`
- Groom rebooking list: cadence by coat, Late/Due tags, consent-aware "Send text", preview of the message. `pawsh-rebooking.png`
- Report card sheet is 10 seconds (mood chips, what we did, line, photo, text preview). `pawsh-reportcard-sheet.png`
- "What we do not keep" and per-vaccine privacy note are the right stance.

## 4. Improvements, ranked
1. **Link appointments to pets** (booking picker, calendar card, prefill from pet page, checkout logs the groom and asks for the next date). Files: appointment schema and booking route in `apps/api/src/routes` / `packages/db/src/schema.ts`, `apps/web/src/app/(dashboard)/m/[merchantId]/calendar`, public booking page, `services/pets.ts`.
2. **Turn the pet features on by default for `pet` vertical signups** (override seed is the only on-switch today). Files: `packages/shared/src/plans.ts`, `apps/api/src/services/merchant.ts`, billing page copy.
3. **Get pets in front of customers**: "Add your dog" after join, wallet link, shop-page blurb for food cards and packs, pet chip on the map and the app. Files: `components/pass/PetLinks.tsx`, shop page, `apps/mobile`.
4. **Pet service template**: size-based prices, add-ons, deposit, no bridal. Files: `apps/api/src/services/merchant.ts` (pet template), seed.
5. **One rebooking, one counter**: hide the generic Rebooking for pet shops; put food cards on the counter. Files: `nav.ts`, counter page.
6. **Fix the run-out consent loop** (P7, P11) and add marketing mentions (P17).

## 5. Self-serve check
- Marketing page to a real owner with a live shop: about 2 minutes (my scripted signup took 9 s of machine time, form has 8 fields; the pet type is hidden under "Something else?").
- Landing page to first point for a customer (my new customer): about 1 minute after the shop exists (shop page, phone, auto-filled code, first name, 50 welcome points).
- To a pet-specific first value: not reachable self-serve. Steps needing help: P1 (admin must enable pet features), P2/P3 (booking and checkout do not know the pet), P5 (customer finds My pets by URL), P7 (reminder consent).
- Remove or default: the Notify-me pages (turn features on), the "Something else?" fold for pets, the cashier consent tick, the per-service Rebooking page.
