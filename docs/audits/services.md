# Audit: service businesses (salon and repair counter)

Reviewer slug: `services`. Date: 2026-10-27. Shops used: Bloom Hair Studio (seeded, 4 chairs worth of calendar), Fix-It Phone Lab (seeded), and a new self-serve salon "Audit Salon 29206". Customers: new random phones (+1647555xxxx). Screenshots: `/tmp/claude-0/-home-user-set-it-up-ai/5a1e2619-40bd-5f60-81fb-50d449557f59/scratchpad/audit/services/` (referenced by number below). Verified facts are what I saw; items marked (opinion) are judgement.

## 1. Verdict

- **Owner:** Bloom-style salon owner would pay $39 for the calendar, deposits, tips and rebooking today if it were buyable; the repair counter would pay $99 for tickets and quotes. But a brand-new salon or shop cannot buy either: the appointment calendar and Café & Retail show "Coming soon" and the Service plan is "ask for early access".
- **Customer:** yes. Booking a haircut, a card-held no-show policy, a quote approved by link and a "ready for pickup" text all worked without a call, on a 390 phone.
- **Single biggest blocker:** self-serve gating. Everything the research doc sells to salons (calendar, booking, rebooking, tips) sits behind a plan that has no checkout and is hidden for new shops (`/m/{id}/calendar` = "Coming with Café & Retail, Notify me").

## 2. What works (keep)

- Calendar (`/m/{id}/calendar`, shot 06, 20): staff columns, now line, open walk-in gaps, "asked for by name", deposit paid shown on the block, status chips, side sheet with last visits. On 390 it collapses to one stylist with a switcher (shot 70). Clear and fast to read.
- Customer booking (`/shops/bloom-hair-studio/book`, shots 10-15): stylist or "Anyone", services with duration, price and "Deposit $40 to hold", only real free times, a plain cancel and no-show policy ("free until 24 hours before, otherwise $25"), phone code sign-in only at the last step, "you are booked" in under a minute. Booking text arrives in the wallet (shot 31).
- Checkout sheet (shot 22-24): add services, apply reward, counter or pay link, 15/20/25% tip presets, "goes to the stylist, not taxed, no points". Done state shows invoice number, points earned and tip.
- Services & prices page has a "Rebook after" column per service; Rebooking page (shot 06-rebooking) shows a preview of the text in 4 languages, STOP and CASL wording, and states a 100% booked-within-7-days result.
- Repair: ticket board with "Needs you now / Waiting on customer / Due today / Ready not collected / Open quote value" (shot 40-tickets), overdue cards with a suggested action, intake with photo slots, condition checklist, data-loss notice, signed drop-off terms, no passcode stored (shot 41-42), price list search, a "final price will not exceed +10%" promise, quote opened/not opened state, customer quote with typed-name signature (shot 50-51), invoice that compares to the approved estimate (shot 64), "ready for pickup" text with status link and a 90-day warranty card (shot 63).
- Drop-off slot booking at `/shops/fix-it-phone-lab/dropoff` shows "2 spots left" and the ticket intake can start from a booked slot.
- Default salon program from signup is sensible: 10 points a visit, 60 points = $10 off a service (shot 90-new, shop page).

## 4. Improvements, ranked

1. **Let any salon switch on the calendar** (S1). Smallest change: a self-serve "Start 30-day trial of Café & Retail" for shops whose vertical is salon, barber or repair, or lower `unlockAtMerchants` for `booking.appointments` and show a Subscribe button. Files: `packages/shared/src/plans.ts`, billing page `apps/web/src/app/(dashboard)/m/[merchantId]/billing`, the locked calendar page, `apps/api/src/routes/billing`.
2. **Salon landing and pricing copy** (S2): one block on `/pricing` and the home slider. Files: `apps/web/src/app/(marketing)/pricing`, home page components.
3. **Salon setup path** (S19, S5, S8): at signup for Salon, ask services (3 presets), hours, HST, and show the booking link and QR. Files: setup page `m/[merchantId]/setup`, `services/page.tsx`.
4. **Name at booking and a Next visit card** (S3, S4): one name field in `apps/web/src/components/pass/BookFlow.tsx`, an upcoming-appointment card in the wallet and shop page, and address/policy on the confirmation.
5. **Rebooking from what the shop already has** (S7): include stamp-only regulars; default Send automatically to a one-tap "Send 5 due". Files: `apps/api/src/services/appointments-booking.ts` (rebooking query), `components/dashboard/service/RebookRules.tsx`.
6. **Receipt and rebook text after payment** (S4, S14): add tip total, receipt link and "book again" link to the payment and ready messages.
7. **Per-dollar points option for salons** (S10).
8. Small copy and polish fixes S6, S9, S12, S13, S16, S17.

## 5. Self-serve check

- Landing page to a created shop: about 2 minutes by form (scripted run: 10 s, all fields typed in one pass). Fields: shop name, name, type, phone, address, neighbourhood, email, password. Nothing needed help.
- Landing to first point earned by a real customer: about 5 minutes for a salon owner on the loyalty path (sign up, open join link, customer enters phone, code, joined and 10 points; verified on the Bloom customer in 1 minute from the shop page).
- Landing to first online booking: **cannot be done self-serve** (S1). On the seeded shop: customer booked, paid and earned points in under 4 minutes end to end.
- Steps that need help: (a) buying or enabling the calendar (blocked), (b) HST number and tax, (c) stylist hours, services and policy (needs the calendar), (d) Stripe onboarding for deposits (not tested), (e) shopper app sign-in in this stack.
- Remove or default: default `tipsEnabled` already by vertical (good); default the booking link into the setup step; default Send automatically for rebooking once the first 5 are reviewed; hide the "Street campaigns", "POS & integrations" items for a one-chair shop until used (opinion).
