# Audit: Desi restaurants (desi-restaurants)

Reviewer role: owner of a family Mississauga/Scarborough biryani house, and a family ordering biryani for Friday pickup.
Stack as run on Oct 27, 2026. Seed shop: nawab-biryani-house (Pro). New shop signed up: "Zaika Karahi 2253" (Scarborough).
Screenshots: `/tmp/claude-0/-home-user-set-it-up-ai/5a1e2619-40bd-5f60-81fb-50d449557f59/scratchpad/audit/desi-restaurants/` (abbreviated `S/` below).
Not verified: the shopper app (:3701) beyond sign-in; its profile step showed "Can't connect right now" in my run (`S/c-app-5.png`), may be stack noise. Stripe card payment (test stub only). WhatsApp/SMS actual sends.

## 1. Verdict
- **Owner:** Would pay for the demo shop as built (kitchen, degh planning, split bill, halal block, four-language menu are real and good). Would NOT be able to pay for it today as a new self-serve owner: after signup and a test-mode purchase of Pro, Orders says "Order-ahead ... Coming with Café & Retail" and the public menu says "No menu online yet".
- **Customer:** Yes. The web path (shop page, menu, add with spice/add-ons, pay at pickup or deposit for a degh, four languages, real RTL Urdu) works on a phone with no app.
- **Biggest blocker:** the restaurant plan is sold but its core feature (commission-free ordering) is switched off for every restaurant that isn't a seeded demo shop, because `orders.order_ahead` is a Café & Retail feature still hidden until 100 shops, and nothing seeds a menu for a new restaurant.

## 4. Improvements (ranked)
1. **Make a restaurant tier actually unlock ordering (D1).** Smallest change: in `isFeatureEnabled`, treat `orders.order_ahead` (and any `effectivePlan`-inherited Café & Retail feature) as GA when `ctx.restaurantTier` is set, or hide the stage gate for restaurants. Files: `packages/shared/src/plans.ts`, `apps/api/src/services/features.ts`, `apps/web/.../orders/page.tsx` gate.
2. **Restaurant-aware signup and first-run (D2).** When type is restaurant: after signup go to a "menu first" setup (languages, halal, add 5 dishes by paste/photo, hours, preview your ordering page). Make the Starter trial start from there, and add a self-serve "Start" CTA on /for-restaurants. Files: `(marketing)/signup/page.tsx`, `(dashboard)/m/[merchantId]/setup/*`, `(marketing)/for-restaurants/*`.
3. **Fix the order and money display bugs (D5, D4)**: balance on #52, banquet card wording, phone-order time parsing. Files: `orders/page.tsx`, `services/orders.ts`, phone-orders parser.
4. **Confirmation page that earns its keep (D8)**: pickup time, address, call, points earned, join. File: `apps/web/src/components/restaurant/OrderView.tsx`.
5. **RTL/script polish (D6, D7)**: `<bdi>` on mixed runs, translate chips, no italics on Arabic script, shared translations for reward and tab names. Files: `KitchenBoard.tsx`, shop page, `MenuBrowser.tsx`, i18n tables.
6. **Rebalance the tiers (D9)**: kitchen display on Growth (pricing.md + `plans.ts` `RESTAURANT_PLANS`), keep analytics/phone assistant on Pro.
7. **Billing page for restaurants (D3)**, WhatsApp header (D11), festival placeholder (D12).

## 5. Self-serve check
- Landing to account: ~8 s of machine time on /signup (form filled by script); a real owner needs 2 to 3 minutes (11 fields, 4 required for the first screen, plus the 6-digit text).
- Landing to first point earned by a real customer: signup, open the join link/QR, enter a phone number, OTP, then stamp from the counter. About 6 to 8 minutes for an owner who follows the setup card; the loyalty path works without help.
- Landing to first **order** by a real customer: not possible without help today (D1, D2): needs us to enable ordering, type or import the menu, and certify halal text. That is a rep visit, not self-serve.
- Steps needing help: menu entry, halal certificate, language names (3 translations x 6 sections), WhatsApp number connection, GST/HST (menu page warns tiffin/catering tax "not set").
- Remove or default: default languages to en+ur+hi+pa with machine-translated names the owner can confirm; default pickup time/cap/deposit; hide Café & Retail and Service cards for restaurants; default cash discount off and explain it in one line; skip "program confirmed" step for restaurants.
