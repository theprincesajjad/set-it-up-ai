# Audit: pharmacies (slug `pharmacies`)

Oct 27, 2026. Reviewer played an Ontario owner-pharmacist (careful about OCP rules and O. Reg. 264/16) and a senior / a parent booking a flu shot. Driven with Playwright against the running stack: new shop `Audit Pharmacy 1835` (self-serve signup) and the seeded `village-apothecary`. Customer at 390 wide. Screenshots in `/tmp/claude-0/-home-user-set-it-up-ai/5a1e2619-40bd-5f60-81fb-50d449557f59/scratchpad/audit/pharmacies/` (called `S/` below). Not legal advice; "College risk" means a point counsel should confirm.

## 1. Verdict
- **Owner:** Would pay $0 happily; would not pay $39 yet. A new (Free) pharmacy gets a safe front-store points card and a fixed pickup text, but no flu-shot booking, no messages: both say "Coming with Café & Retail", which opens at 100 shops (37 today). Only the seeded pilot shop has them.
- **Customer:** A senior will use the card; the reward is thin (1 point per $1, $5 off per $500 of front-store spend). A parent can book a flu shot in about 5 taps, but only at a pilot shop.
- **Biggest blocker:** the pharmacy value story (flu-shot booking, reminders) is behind an unreleased plan, while the guard rules have gaps a pharmacist could get in trouble for (visit-based welcome points, an unenforced pharmacist confirmation, a word-list text check).

## 2. What works (keep) — all seen
- Earning is front-store only in practice: counter refuses Award until "This total has no prescriptions or pharmacy services" is ticked ("Confirm the total has no prescriptions or pharmacy services."); the tick is required every sale. Reports say they count front-store sales only (`S/new-reports.png`).
- Locked categories: Prescriptions, Pharmacy services, Dispensing fees and compounding show a black "Locked" tag, a "Why?" and the regulation cited. New categories typed as "Prescription refills", "Immunizations", "Blister packs", "Compounding", "Medication packaging", "Pharmacist services" auto-locked (`S/new-categories.png`).
- Texts: composer blocks "10% off your prescriptions", "Flu shot reminder: time for your yearly vaccine", "Refill your Lipitor" with "This can't be sent" and disables Schedule (`S/va-messages-blocked.png`).
- No referral reward, no review request, no notes/tags on customers; Rewards page lists "Also blocked for this shop".
- Appointment page for the shopper: no price, no notes field, "No notes, no health details. We only record that you booked. Bring your health card." and the exact reminder text is shown before it is sent (`S/cust-book6.png`). Language switch (English, Hindi, Urdu, Punjabi) on every shop page.
- Pickup ready: one fixed text ("Village Apothecary: your order is ready for pickup. Reply STOP to opt out."), needs a consent tick, number masked after send (`S/va-pickup-sent.png`).
- Signup is short (one form, "Pharmacy" is a choice) and the program is pre-filled with no choices to make (`S/signup-filled.png`, `S/after-signup.png`).
