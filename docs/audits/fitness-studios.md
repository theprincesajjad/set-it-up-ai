# Audit: fitness studios (yoga, spin, boxing)

Reviewed 2026-10-27 on the shared stack. Owner = Emma at Rise Yoga Studio (seeded, Roncesvalles) plus a new self-signed shop "Fit Test Yoga 4362". Customers = fresh `+1647555xxxx` phones at 390px width. Screenshots: `/tmp/claude-0/-home-user-set-it-up-ai/5a1e2619-40bd-5f60-81fb-50d449557f59/scratchpad/audit/fitness-studios/`. Facts marked "verified" were seen in the running product. Opinions are marked.

## 1. Verdict
- **Owner:** Not today as a self-serve purchase. The class module is excellent once it is switched on for the seeded studio, but a new studio that signs up cannot turn it on (page says "Coming with Café & Retail"; pricing says "ask for early access"), and the pricing page never mentions classes, memberships or the 1% class fee.
- **Customer:** Yes. A member can book, waitlist, join with an agreement and cancel inside the cooling-off window in under two minutes on a phone. The loyalty reward (a free drop-in) is worth little to the people a studio most wants to keep.
- **Biggest blocker:** self-serve. A new studio cannot get Class schedule, Memberships & passes or Door check-in without an admin override (F1).

## 2. What works (keep)
- Auto-promotion works (verified by API test): class of 2, A and B booked, C waitlisted, A cancelled before the window, C was booked automatically and the roster showed it.
- Late cancel works: inside the 12-hour window the credit was forfeited (`late_cancelled`) and the roster shows Late cancel / Excuse.
- Door check-in is fast: phone lookup checked in a booked member (+10 points), added an unbooked member with a usable pass ("Added and checked in"), and stopped a no-pass person with a clear red line.
- The booking sheet puts the cancel rule, late fee and studio note above the button; the waitlist confirmation says "number 4... we book you automatically".
- The membership agreement is plain English with price + HST = total, first and next payment dates, the 10-day refund date, freeze, late-cancel and renewal. Paying needs the checkbox. Cooling-off cancel is one tap and says "$158.20 goes back to your card". The Passes card shows price, next payment, card, and a red but quiet Cancel.
- Owner pages are calm and readable: week schedule with fill bars and "+3 waitlist", roster showing how each person paid, a Class rules sheet with the Ontario notice hint, and a Members tab with Payment failed, Frozen and "Ends Nov 1" states.
- No horizontal overflow on any page at 390px (customer pages and the owner schedule, roster and door).
- Women-only studio data is respected: phones are masked on staff screens ("(647) ***-4210").

## 4. Answers to the specific questions

**Is the 1% class fee fair?** (opinion with the numbers) Yes for the small studio, and it is lower than the alternatives in the research. Examples before Stripe: $3,000 a month online costs about $69 all in; 100 unlimited members at $140 ($14,000) costs $39 + $140 = $179. Mindbody starts around US$99 to $139 and Walla at $320, so a small independent wins, and a studio near $20,000 online still pays about $239. Risks: it grows with the studio's success and has no ceiling; it applies to late-fee charges, so the studio pays us when it penalises a no-show; it is not mentioned on the pricing page (F2), and I did not verify whether the 1% is returned when the 10-day refund happens. Desk sales at 0% are a fair way to avoid forcing the fee on cash studios. Suggest a cap (for example $99 a month total) and showing it on /pricing.

**Does loyalty help a studio?** (opinion) Partly. Points per visit work for drop-in and pack buyers: 10 visits earns one $28 class, about 10% back, and the point total at check-in ("+10 points, 40 in all") is a nice moment. They do almost nothing for members. A member who books three classes a week earns 120 points a month for a drop-in they will never need. Retention in the research is decided in the first 60 to 90 days, so the reward that matters is "come back four times in month one" and a milestone (10th, 25th, 50th class), not a free single class. Also the loyalty enrolment happens at check-in, not at booking, so the first-timer's wallet is empty until they attend.

1. **Make the module self-serve** (F1, F2). Smallest change: let a studio flip `booking.classes` and `memberships.recurring` on from Billing & plans (trial or pilot toggle), add Gym / studio to the visible signup types and a studio row on /pricing. 3. **Door and roster actions** (F4, F6): sell a drop-in from the door result, allow staff, add Cancel booking and "Book in now". Files: `apps/web/src/components/dashboard/classes/`, `apps/api/src/services/classes.ts`, `routes/classes/index.ts`.

5. **First-timer path** (F8, F9, F7): suggest the free first class in the booking sheet, enrol at first booking, warn waitlisters without a pass, and a class-count milestone reward in the studio template. Files: `ClassSchedule.tsx`, `seed/classes.ts` and the fitness vertical template.

7. **Cap the 1% fee** and publish it (section 4).

## 6. Self-serve check
- Landing page to a created studio shop: about 1 minute by script (one form, no help needed). A person would take about 2 minutes. Nothing blocked.
- Landing page to first point earned by a customer **on a new studio**: the stamp path takes about 3 to 5 minutes through the setup guide (stamp yourself), but it is the generic loyalty path. A new studio cannot sell or book a class at all, so the module's time-to-value is "never without a call" (F1).
- On the seeded studio (where the module exists): customer phone to first booked free class took about 90 seconds on the web (code, free first class, book). To first points: they arrive when the owner checks them in at the door, not at booking.
- Steps that need help: enabling the module (admin override), Stripe (the stack's fake provider was shown as "Test payment (development)"; real Stripe onboarding was not tested), and legal wording (counsel, per the research).
- Remove or default: show Gym / studio as a first-level choice, preload a starter schedule and the three offers (drop-in, 10-pack, unlimited) from the template, and default the rules (12 hours, $0 fee) as the research says. Rise's seeded $10 fee is a demo value, so note that the default for a new studio is still $0.
